# snake_player_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Simple Streaming at scale. Uiza is the complete toolkit for building a powerful video streaming application with unlimited scalability. We design Uiza so simple that you only need a few lines of codes to start streaming, but sophisticated enough for you to build complex products on top of it.
