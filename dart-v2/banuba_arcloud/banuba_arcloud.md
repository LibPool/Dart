# banuba_arcloud

**Tag**: augmented-reality, ar-effects, cloud, sdk, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Banuba AR Cloud for Flutter. Load and update Banuba Face AR effects from the cloud at runtime, so apps can add or change AR effects without shipping a new build.
