# google_fonts_cyrillic_ext_base

**Tag**: web, mobile, networking

## 简介

Cyrillic Extended fonts by google right into your Flutter application. Contains Regular, RegularItalic, Bold and BoldItalic styles of fonts. Taken from https://fonts.google.com.
