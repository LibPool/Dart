# youtube_dl

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin to download videos from YouTube.com and a few more sites. Based on youtube-dl executable & youtubedl-android library.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/f0rx/youtube_dl
- 问题追踪: https://github.com/f0rx/youtube_dl/issues
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/youtube_dl
