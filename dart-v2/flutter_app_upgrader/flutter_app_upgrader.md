# flutter_app_upgrader

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A Flutter plugin for upgrading the app on Android by installing after downloading the new apk or IOS by going to the App Store.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/flyaswind/flutter_app_upgrader
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/flutter_app_upgrader
