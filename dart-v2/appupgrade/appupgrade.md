# appupgrade

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

appupgrade - Flutter App Upgrade Package. 一个Flutter应用检查更新及应用升级的插件包，参考：flutter_app_upgrade , 本插件Android使用Java编写，iOS使用OC编写。
