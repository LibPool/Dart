# ve_sdk_flutter

**Tag**: video-editor, video-editing, sdk, ar, camera, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

White-label video editor SDK for Flutter. Add a full-featured video editing UI to iOS and Android apps: multi-track timeline, AI clipping, AI captions, video templates, drafts, stickers, AR effects and background removal.
