# agora_uikit

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter plugin to simply integrate Agora Video Calling or Live Video Streaming to your app with just a few lines of code.

## 官网

- 主页: https://www.agora.io/en/
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/AgoraIO-Community/VideoUIKit-Flutter
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/agora_uikit
