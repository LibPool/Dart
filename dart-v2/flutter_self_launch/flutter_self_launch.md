# flutter_self_launch

**Tag**: android, scheduling, alarm, self-launch, background, mobile, state-management

## 简介

A lightweight Flutter package for scheduling Android app launches from terminated state.
Uses minimal permissions (WAKE_LOCK, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) with AlarmManager for reliable scheduling.
