# multi_thumb_slider

**Tag**: ui

## 简介

A slider with multiple thumbs. The behavior of the slider is similar to the official Slider widget. The MultiThumbSlider should have at least 3 thumbs. For less thumbs, use Slider or the RangeSlider.
