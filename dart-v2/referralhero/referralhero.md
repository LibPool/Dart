# referralhero

**Tag**: mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

ReferralHero is a powerful Flutter package that allows you to effortlessly create viral, lead-generating, and revenue-boosting referral programs for your business.With ReferralHero, building a referral program becomes easy, empowering your customers to become enthusiastic brand ambassadors. Experience substantial business growth as your referral program fuels exponential growth, helping you reach new customers and increase revenue.
