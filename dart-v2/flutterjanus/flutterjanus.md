# flutterjanus

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for Janus Gateway. FlutterJanus is a port of janus.js in flutter, the functionality of janus.js is reproduced in flutterjanus.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/maanasnetwork/flutter_janus
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/flutterjanus
