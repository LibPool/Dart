# open_ad_flutter

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A Plugin that allows to integrate the new App Open Ads (by Google AdMob) in your Flutter Projects.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/mattcianna/open_ad_flutter
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/open_ad_flutter

## 历史版本号

- 1.0.0 (2020-12-18)

## 获取地址

- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/open_ad_flutter
- pub 安装: `dart pub add open_ad_flutter`
- Flutter 安装: `flutter pub add open_ad_flutter`

- 最新版归档: https://pub.dev/api/archives/open_ad_flutter-1.0.0.tar.gz
