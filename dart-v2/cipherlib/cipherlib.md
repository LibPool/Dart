# cipherlib

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Pure-Dart symmetric ciphers (AES modes, ChaCha20, XChaCha20, Salsa20, XSalsa20), their Poly1305 AEAD variants, and ML-KEM post-quantum key encapsulation (FIPS 203).
