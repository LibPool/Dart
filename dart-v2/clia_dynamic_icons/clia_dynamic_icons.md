# clia_dynamic_icons

**Tag**: mobile, tooling

## 简介

Insert Material Icon and FontAwesome icons dynamically in Flutter app when the icons are not known at compile time.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/clia/clia_dynamic_icon
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/clia_dynamic_icons
