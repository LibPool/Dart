# agora_rtc_engine

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin of Agora RTC SDK, allow you to simply integrate Agora Video Calling or Live Video Streaming to your app with just a few lines of code.

## 官网

- 主页: https://www.agora.io
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/AgoraIO-Extensions/Agora-Flutter-SDK/tree/main
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/agora_rtc_engine
