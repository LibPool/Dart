# appinio_social_share_plus

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Supports sharing text, images, files and text with image to social media (Facebook, Instagram, Instagram Story, Messenger, Telegram, WhatsApp, Twitter, Tiktok, SMS, System, etc.).
