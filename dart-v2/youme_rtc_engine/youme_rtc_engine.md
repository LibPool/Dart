# youme_rtc_engine

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin of Youme RTC SDK, allow you to simply integrate Youme Video Calling or Live Video Chat to your app with just a few lines of code.

- 主页: https://youme.im/
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/youmesdk/rtc-flutter-plugin
