# zhuge

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

This is the official flutter plugin for Zhugeio,with this plugin you can easily collect your app data on Android and iOS.

## 官网

- 主页: http://gl.zhugeio.com/zhukang/Zhuge.git
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/zhuge
