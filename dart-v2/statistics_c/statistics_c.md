## 简介

This is  a statistics package for flutter.You can find  Mean, Median, Quartiles, Deciles, Percentiles, Range, Coefficient of Range, Quartiles Deviation, Coefficient of Quartiles Deviation, Standard Deviation, Coefficient of Varience.
