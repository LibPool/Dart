# flutter_push_notify

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

The "flutter_push_notify" package provides an easy-to-use solution for managing push notifications in your Flutter app. Group multiple notifications into a single notification to avoid clutter and customize notifications with images, priority levels, and user actions. A must-have for any developer looking to provide a streamlined and personalized push notification experience to their users.
