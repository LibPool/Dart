# serialport_for_msp

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Fork of flutter_libserialport 0.5.0 migrated to the
Flutter v2 plugin embedding (no Registrar), minSdk 21 and compileSdk 35.
Published temporarily for MSP-based apps that need a working
serial-port plugin on Flutter 3.24+.
