# qr_flutter_plus

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

QR Flutter Plus — a modern, maintained fork for fast QR code rendering via Widget or CustomPainter. Built on qr.dart with gradient, styled eyes/modules, and embedded image support. No internet required.
