# flutter_nordic_dfu

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

This library allows you to do a Device Firmware Update (DFU) of your nrf51 or nrf52 chip from Nordic Semiconductor. Currently, it only works for Android.

- 主页: https://github.com/fengqiangboy/flutter-nordic-dfu
