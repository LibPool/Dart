# enough_icalendar

**Tag**: icalendar, ics, ical, rfc5545, rfc5546

## 简介

iCalendar library to parse, generate and respond to iCal / ics invites. Fully compliant with RFC 5545 (iCalendar) and RFC 5546 (iTIP).
