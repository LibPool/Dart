# pushstate_anchor

**Tag**: security, state-management

## 简介

Extend the <a> tag with history.pushState(). Dart translation of pushstate-anchor that is compatible with Polymer.dart. Original author is Erik Ringsmuth <erik.ringsmuth@gmail.com>.
