# htmlencodedtext

**Tag**: web, mobile

## 简介

A Flutter plugin that makes it easier to show HTML Encoded Text (from outside APIs perhaps) in your Material Flutter apps.

## 官网

- 主页: http://spk.design
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/htmlencodedtext
