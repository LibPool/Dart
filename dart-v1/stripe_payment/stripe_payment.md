# stripe_payment

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A Flutter plugin to integrate the stripe libraries for iOS and Android. Supports Apple / Google Pay, SCA, PSD2 and much more.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/jonasbark/flutter_stripe_payment
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/stripe_payment
