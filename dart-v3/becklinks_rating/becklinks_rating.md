# becklinks_rating

**Tag**: in-app-review, app-rating, rating-prompt, beck-links, mobile

## 简介

Ask for a store review at the right moment in Flutter apps on Android and iOS: thresholds set in the Beck Links dashboard, rules applied on the device, no tracking.
