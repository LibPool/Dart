# pqcrypto

**Tag**: cryptography, post-quantum, security, dart, flutter, mobile

## 简介

Pure Dart post-quantum cryptography. Zero dependencies. ML-KEM, ML-DSA, and SLH-DSA with checked-in NIST vector evidence.
