# moonx_flutter

**Tag**: wallet, web3, mpc, passkeys, web, mobile, ui, security

## 简介

MoonX embedded wallets and authentication for Flutter apps on iOS and Android. Headless, so your app owns the UI, with native passkeys and MPC signing.
