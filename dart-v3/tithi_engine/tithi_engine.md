# tithi_engine

**Tag**: astronomy, calendar, hindu-calendar, tithi

## 简介

Accurate Vedic tithi, lunar month, and festival date calculator for any city worldwide. Pure Dart, no dependencies, 200-year accuracy (1900–2100).
