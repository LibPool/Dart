# nudge

**Tag**: engagement, analytics, nudge, triggers, rating, mobile

## 简介

A local-first nudge engine for Flutter. Log behavioural events on device, describe conditions with a fluent DSL and fire rate prompts, upsells and tips at the right moment, with cooldowns, caps, outcomes and chaining.
