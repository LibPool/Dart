# resonance_sheet

**Tag**: widget, ui, overlay, animation, modal, mobile

## 简介

Spring-driven bottom, top, and center modal sheets for Flutter with size-adaptive entrance overshoot, coordinated scroll-to-dismiss, and an iOS-style overscroll rubber-band cue.
