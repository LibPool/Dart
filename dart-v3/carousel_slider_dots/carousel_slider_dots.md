# carousel_slider_dots

**Tag**: carousel, slider, carousel-slider, page-indicator, dots-indicator, mobile

## 简介

Carousel slider for Flutter with a smooth animated dots indicator. Auto play, infinite scroll, enlarge center page, vertical slider, controller and fully customizable page indicator.
