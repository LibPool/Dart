# gmaps_vehicle_tracker

**Tag**: google-maps, maps, tracking, location, animation, ui

## 简介

Smooth, road-aware live vehicle tracking on Google Maps: route matching, bounded prediction, camera follow, built-in vehicle markers and an Uber-style vehicle selector.
