# smf_firebase_messaging

**Tag**: flutter, firebase, push-notifications, firebase-messaging, template, mobile

## 简介

The SMF module of Firebase Cloud Messaging for the Flutter app that SMF generates. The app receives push messages and hears of the taps on them.
