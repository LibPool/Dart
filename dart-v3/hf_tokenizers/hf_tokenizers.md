# hf_tokenizers

**Tag**: llm, ai, tokenizer, ffi, nlp, serialization

## 简介

HuggingFace tokenizers for Dart over FFI. Load any tokenizer.json and get byte-exact BPE, WordPiece, and Unigram encoding, backed by the Rust crate.
