# mapkit_flutter

**Tag**: map, ios, macos, apple, flutter-plugin, mobile, desktop, testing

## 简介

MapKit for Flutter — MKMapView on iOS and macOS with Apple's exact symbol names: annotations, clustering, overlays, tile overlays, Look Around, snapshots, mockable controller.
