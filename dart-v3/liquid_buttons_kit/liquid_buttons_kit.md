# liquid_buttons_kit

**Tag**: widget, ui, buttons, animation, mobile

## 简介

Spring-driven liquid-style buttons and press surfaces for Flutter — press-to-scale springs with press-through gestures, a finger-following glow overlay, and solid outlined shapes.
