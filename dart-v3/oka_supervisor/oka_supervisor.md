# oka_supervisor

**Tag**: supervisor, lifecycle, processes, declarative, web, state-management

## 简介

Declarative supervisor (ADR-0040): steady-state convergence over the resource_composition substrate. Desired state is declared as typed component specs; a pure planner diffs desired against observed; a crash-only converge driver acts once and exits — budgeted restarts, cession-based ownership, and an advisory machine registry. No daemon, no second watcher, no output interpretation.
