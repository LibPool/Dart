# natlas_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter SDK for N-ATLaS — Nigeria's sovereign multilingual LLM. Chat, streaming, voice transcription, and ready-made widgets for Hausa, Igbo, Yorùbá, and Nigerian English.
