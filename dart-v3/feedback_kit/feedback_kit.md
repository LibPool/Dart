# feedback_kit

**Tag**: mobile, ui, security, state-management

## 简介

In-app feedback boards, voting and a public roadmap for Flutter. Auth-agnostic: bring your own identity via a FeedbackIdentityProvider, or let the built-in anonymous device identity handle it. Pairs with the platform Feedback service.
