# wikha_video_player

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

A highly polished, feature-rich Flutter video player with Netflix-style UI, supporting Mux/HLS streams, multi-track audio/subtitles, live streaming, storyboard previews, and Picture-in-Picture.
