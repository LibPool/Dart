# dual_camera_plus_flutter

**Tag**: camera, dual-camera, video-recording, metal, camerax, mobile

## 简介

Simultaneous front and back dual camera recording and photo capture with hardware-accelerated live preview, composited layouts, and focus/exposure control for Flutter.
