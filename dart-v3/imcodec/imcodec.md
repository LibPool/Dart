# imcodec

**Tag**: image, image-processing, codec, compression, flutter, web, mobile

## 简介

Focused BMP, GIF, JPEG, JPEG XL, OpenEXR, PNG, QOI, TGA, TIFF, and WebP codecs for RGBA images in Flutter.
