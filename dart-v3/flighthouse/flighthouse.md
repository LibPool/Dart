# flighthouse

**Tag**: accessibility, a11y, performance, lighthouse, testing, mobile

## 简介

Flutter accessibility, performance, Lighthouse-style report, and CI gate. Merges attest, Lighthouse, axe, and integration_test results into one scored report with a baseline diff.
