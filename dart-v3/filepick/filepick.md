# filepick

**Tag**: image-picker, file-picker, gallery, camera, crop, mobile

## 简介

WhatsApp and Telegram style media and attachment picker for Flutter: an in-app gallery sheet with a Camera tile first, albums, a Files button, photo cropping, native installed apps query, and an upload progress ring.
