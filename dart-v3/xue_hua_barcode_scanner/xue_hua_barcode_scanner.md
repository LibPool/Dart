# xue_hua_barcode_scanner

**Tag**: barcode, barcode-scanner, qrcode, zxing-cpp, ffi, web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

A 6-platform (Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux, Web) Flutter Barcode & QR Code recognition, generation, and real-time camera scanner plugin powered by zxing-cpp FFI & WebAssembly.
