# yatmo_sdk

**Tag**: maps, maplibre, real-estate, geolocation, web, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Yatmo neighbourhood intelligence for Flutter real-estate apps, a MapLibre map with points of interest, travel times and neighbourhood summaries.
