# structured_log_riverpod

**Tag**: logging, riverpod, state-management, structured-logging, debugging, web, mobile

## 简介

StructuredLogRiverpodObserver — a ProviderObserver that turns the lifecycle of every Riverpod provider (created, updated, failed, removed) into structured_log entries. Works with flutter_riverpod as is.
