# analog_vu_meter

**Tag**: audio, meter, gauge, widget, mobile, ui

## 简介

Analog needle VU meters for Flutter. Six faces, four frames and analog, digital (dBFS) or custom scales, freely combinable. Pure Dart painting, no assets, no dependencies.
