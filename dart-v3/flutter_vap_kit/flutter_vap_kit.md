# flutter_vap_kit

**Tag**: vap, animation, video, alpha-video, gift, mobile, ui, networking

## 简介

Play Tencent VAP transparent (alpha-channel) MP4 animations with one widget: seamless looping, automatic first-frame poster, asset and network caching.
