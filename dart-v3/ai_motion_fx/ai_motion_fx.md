# ai_motion_fx

**Tag**: animation, ai, loading, indicator, custom-painter, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Animated AI status indicators for Flutter, drawn with CustomPainter: nine thinking-orb states, image and voice orbs, plus glow effects that wrap your own widgets (sound-reactive voice glow, border beam, image reveal loader).
