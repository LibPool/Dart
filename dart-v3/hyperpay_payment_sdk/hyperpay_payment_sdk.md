# hyperpay_payment_sdk

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

A Flutter plugin for seamless HyperPay payment gateway integration. Supports ReadyUI & CustomUI, VISA, MasterCard, MADA, STC Pay, and Apple Pay on Android & iOS.
