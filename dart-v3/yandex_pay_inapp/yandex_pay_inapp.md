# yandex_pay_inapp

**Tag**: yandex, payments, fintech, inapp, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Yandex Pay InApp module for Flutter. Adds the in-app payment flow (pay widget + availability state) on top of yandex_pay_core. Requires the redirect flow.
