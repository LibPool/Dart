# swift_transitions

**Tag**: transition, animation, navigation, cupertino, ios, mobile, ui

## 简介

iOS's navigation transitions for Flutter: the iOS 18 zoom transition with its interactive dismissal, and the push, both fitted frame by frame against the real thing.
