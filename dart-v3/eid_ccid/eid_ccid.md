# eid_ccid

**Tag**: eid, smartcard, pcsc, ccid, identity, mobile, desktop

## 简介

Reads electronic identity cards through a USB or PC/SC card reader, on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux. The eid transport for the ccid plugin.
