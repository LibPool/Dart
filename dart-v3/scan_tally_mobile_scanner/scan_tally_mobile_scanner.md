# scan_tally_mobile_scanner

**Tag**: barcode, scanner, inventory, camera, mobile-scanner, mobile

## 简介

mobile_scanner camera adapter for scan_tally: continuous barcode counting with duplicate filtering, frame confirmation, undo and GS1 parsing.
