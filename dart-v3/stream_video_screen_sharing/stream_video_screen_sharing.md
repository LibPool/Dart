# stream_video_screen_sharing

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Official Screen Sharing Plugin for Stream Video Flutter that enables sharing device screens during video calls.

## 官网

- 主页: https://getstream.io/video/
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/GetStream/stream-video-flutter
