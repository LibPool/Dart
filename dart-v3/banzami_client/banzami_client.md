# banzami_client

**Tag**: payments, fintech, checkout, angola, web, networking, tooling

## 简介

Public client SDK for the Banzami payment network — present a hosted checkout, read a payment's public status, and parse Banzami QR and deep links. Uses a publishable key; money movement stays on your server.
