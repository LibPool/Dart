# mx_merchant

**Tag**: payments, mxmerchant, checkout, rest, gateway, web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

A Flutter/Dart client package for integrating with the MX Merchant REST APIs, including checkout, payments, customers, and vaulted accounts.
