# flutter_ppg

**Tag**: ppg, heart-rate, hrv, camera, health, mobile

## 简介

A Flutter package for camera-based PPG (Photoplethysmography) signal processing. Extracts RR intervals from camera frames for heart rate and HRV analysis.
