# reactive_bloub

**Tag**: avatar, mascot, animation, character, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

An animated, procedurally-rendered blob mascot avatar for Flutter — 14 shapes, 16 expressions, and 15 animated states, all cross-fading live.
