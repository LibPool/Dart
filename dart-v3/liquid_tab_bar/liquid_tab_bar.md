# liquid_tab_bar

**Tag**: navigation, tabs, glassmorphism, ios, ui, mobile

## 简介

Two iOS 26-style floating liquid-glass tab bars for Flutter — a grabbable soap-bubble lens, or a droplet variant with search, actions and badges.
