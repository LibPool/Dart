# axevpn_flutter

**Tag**: vpn, openvpn, wireguard, v2ray, networking, mobile

## 简介

Multi-protocol VPN plugin for Flutter supporting OpenVPN, WireGuard, V2Ray/Xray (VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks) and OpenConnect on Android and iOS.
