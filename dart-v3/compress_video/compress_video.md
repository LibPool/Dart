# compress_video

**Tag**: video, compression, transcoding, media, video-compression, mobile, desktop

## 简介

Compress phone-recorded video to a smaller MP4 that plays everywhere, on Android, iOS and macOS, with typed media info and thumbnails. The drop-in successor to video_compress.
