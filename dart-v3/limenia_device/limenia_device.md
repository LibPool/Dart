# limenia_device

**Tag**: moderation, trust-and-safety, play-integrity, devicecheck, mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for the device connector of Limenia: gets a token of the device from Play Integrity (Android) or DeviceCheck (iOS) for the app to send to its own backend.
