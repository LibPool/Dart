# liquid_glass_bottom_navbar_plus

**Tag**: liquid-glass, glassmorphism, navigation-bar, bottom-bar, shader, mobile, ui

## 简介

A drop-in Flutter bottom bar with Apple iOS 26 Liquid Glass — refraction, specular highlights, fluid morph and scroll-minimize, with a frosted fallback.
