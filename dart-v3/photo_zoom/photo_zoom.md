# photo_zoom

**Tag**: image, zoom, gallery, viewer, gestures, desktop

## 简介

Pan, zoom, rotate and swipe a photo gallery. Double tap and pinch zoom where you touch; mouse wheel on desktop. A type-safe, maintained alternative to photo_view.
