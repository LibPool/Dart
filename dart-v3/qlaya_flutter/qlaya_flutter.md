# qlaya_flutter

**Tag**: flutter, machine-learning, onnx, ai, inference, mobile, tooling

## 简介

QLaya Flutter & Dart client for the fast on-device decision engine powered by the qlaya.int8.onnx model for classification, scoring, verification, and routing tasks.
