# universal_storage_world

**Tag**: web, mobile

## 简介

App- and game-agnostic world model over the convergence kernel (ADR 0047): stable member addressing (WorldUrn), interest policies for zone journeys, zone catalogs, the member-codec seam, journey phases, and the host-driven pulse contract. Pure Dart: no Flutter, no ecsly, no harness, no transports.
