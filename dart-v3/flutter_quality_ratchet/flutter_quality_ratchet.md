# flutter_quality_ratchet

**Tag**: lint, ci, code-quality, flutter, architecture, mobile, tooling

## 简介

A quality ratchet for Dart and Flutter projects: freeze today's lint debt in a baseline that may only shrink, and fail CI on every new violation.
