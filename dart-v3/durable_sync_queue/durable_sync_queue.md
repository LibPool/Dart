# durable_sync_queue

**Tag**: offline, sync, queue, retry, idempotency

## 简介

A persistent, ordered, retrying operation queue for offline-first apps. Idempotency keys, per-group ordering, exponential backoff and dead-lettering, with pluggable storage. Pure Dart.
