# sri_lanka_nic

**Tag**: sri-lanka, nic, validation, identity

## 简介

Parse, validate, decode, and convert Sri Lankan NIC numbers. Pure Dart, zero dependencies, typed errors, honest about the unpublished check-digit algorithm.
