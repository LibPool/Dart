# kugelaudio

**Tag**: tts, text-to-speech, speech, audio, voice, web, mobile, networking

## 简介

Official KugelAudio SDK for Dart and Flutter: low-latency text to speech (REST and WebSocket streaming), voices, dictionaries and speech enhancement.
