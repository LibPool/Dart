# keycaps

**Tag**: keyboard, shortcuts, widgets, ui, mobile

## 简介

Keycap and keyboard-shortcut widgets for Flutter. Draws ⌘⇧P or Ctrl+Shift+P from a string, a LogicalKeyboardKey or a ShortcutActivator, themed and labelled per platform.
