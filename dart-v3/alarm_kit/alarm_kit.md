# alarm_kit

**Tag**: alarm, timer, ios, live-activities, alarmkit, mobile

## 简介

AlarmKit for Flutter: alarms and timers on iOS and iPadOS 26 that ring through silent mode and Focus, with the system's countdown Live Activity.
