# curved_dual_tab_bar

**Tag**: tab-bar, widget, animation, ui

## 简介

Curved dual-segment tab bar with an animated S-curve divider, built on a real Material TabBar — drop-in DEPOSIT/WITHDRAW style headers with full color/gradient/border theming.
