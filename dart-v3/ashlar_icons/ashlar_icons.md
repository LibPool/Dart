# ashlar_icons

**Tag**: icons, icon-font, ui, mobile

## 简介

6,553 icons in ten styles (Rounded, Standard and Sharp; stroke, solid, duotone, twotone, bulk) as Flutter icon fonts whose codepoints never change.
