# nn_youtube_player

**Tag**: youtube, video, player, course, learning, mobile

## 简介

Professional Flutter YouTube player with learning mode, anti-skip protection, watch progress tracking, completion validation, playback speed controls, resume playback, fullscreen support, and custom Material 3 controls.
