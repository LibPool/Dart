# free_paging

**Tag**: pull-to-refresh, load-more, pagination, infinite-scroll, list, web, mobile

## 简介

Pure Dart pagination container for Flutter with pull-to-refresh, load-more, empty and error views, local paging and caching. Same behaviour on iOS, Android, HarmonyOS and Web.
