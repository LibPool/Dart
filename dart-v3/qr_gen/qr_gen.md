# qr_gen

**Tag**: qr, qrcode, barcode, widget, svg, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

A maintained QR code generator. Pure-Dart ISO/IEC 18004 encoder (all 40 versions, L/M/Q/H, auto segmentation) plus a Flutter widget, painter and SVG.
