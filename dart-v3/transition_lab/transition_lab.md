# transition_lab

**Tag**: animation, transition, navigation, page-transition, ui, mobile

## 简介

Page transitions re-created from well-known apps: the Wolt swing, iOS zoom and sheet, Instagram cube, photo drag to dismiss, page curl, doors, mini-player.
