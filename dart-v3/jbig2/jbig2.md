# jbig2

**Tag**: jbig2, image, codec, pdf

## 简介

Pure Dart JBIG2 codec: decodes standalone files and the embedded streams PDF's /JBIG2Decode filter carries, and encodes bi-level bitmaps back to either form. Runs on the VM, dart2js and dart2wasm.
