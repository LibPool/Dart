# ember_streak

**Tag**: streak, habit, gamification, animation, widget, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

A streak counter for Flutter: a procedurally drawn flickering flame, a count that pops, a week of days that fill in, milestone sparks and an at-risk state.
