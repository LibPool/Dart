# watad_maps

**Tag**: maps, iraq, maplibre, geocoding, arabic, mobile

## 简介

WATAD Maps for Flutter. The map of Iraq with WATAD's style, icons and Arabic, Kurdish and English names, plus search, nearby places, addresses and directions, from one key.
