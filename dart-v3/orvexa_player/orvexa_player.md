# orvexa_player

**Tag**: video, video-player, hls, vast, picture-in-picture, mobile, ui

## 简介

A Flutter MP4/HLS video player with customizable controls, fullscreen, video and Widget ads, VAST/VMAP, and opt-in native picture in picture.
