# just_sonner

**Tag**: toast, notification, snackbar, overlay, desktop, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Stacking toasts for Flutter desktop in the spirit of sonner: a deck that fans out on hover, toasts updated in place, promise toasts, no BuildContext.
