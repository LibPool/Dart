# zunia_sdk

**Tag**: wallet, cosmos, blockchain, qr-code, deep-linking, mobile, security, state-management

## 简介

Connect Flutter apps to the Zunia wallet: QR pairing and deep links over zunia.connect.v2, end-to-end encrypted, with Cosmos signing and sign-in.
