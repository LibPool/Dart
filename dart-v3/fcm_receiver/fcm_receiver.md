# fcm_receiver

**Tag**: mobile, security

## 简介

Receive Firebase Cloud Messaging pushes from Flutter on any platform by speaking the Google MCS protocol, with pure-Dart in-process decryption of encrypted payloads. No firebase_messaging, no native binary.
