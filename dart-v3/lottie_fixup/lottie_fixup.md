# lottie_fixup

**Tag**: lottie, animation, after-effects, cli, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Fixes Lottie/Bodymovin exports that crash or freeze the lottie Flutter package: malformed layers, assets, masks, shapes, and unexecuted expressions.
