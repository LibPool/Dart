# vorbis_decoder_ffi

**Tag**: audio, codec, vorbis, ffi, mobile, desktop

## 简介

Ogg Vorbis decoder for Flutter on Android, iOS, and macOS, powered by stb_vorbis through FFI. Decodes in-memory Ogg streams to interleaved 16-bit PCM.
