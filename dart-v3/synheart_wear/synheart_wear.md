# synheart_wear

**Tag**: wearable, biosignals, flutter, health, hrv, mobile

## 简介

Flutter SDK for wearable signals — Apple HealthKit, Health Connect, WHOOP, Garmin, Oura, and Fitbit. Unified HR, HRV, steps, calories, distance, stress.
