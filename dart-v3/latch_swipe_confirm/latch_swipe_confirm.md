# latch_swipe_confirm

**Tag**: button, swipe, confirm, animation, widget, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

A swipe-to-confirm button for Flutter: resistance at the start, a latch point, an async confirm with loading, success and failure states, and accessible alternatives to swiping.
