# universal_rating_bar

**Tag**: rating, rating-bar, star-rating, widgets, svg, mobile, ui

## 简介

A flexible Flutter rating bar with icon, SVG/PNG asset, and custom widget support, half ratings, drag gestures, and read-only mode.
