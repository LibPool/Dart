# healthypi_healthy_store

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart client for the ProtoCentral Healthy Store (HPI_HS), the MCUmgr group 0x1000 on HealthyPi Move. Cursor-based sample sync, a self-describing metric registry, and CRC-verified raw-signal records. Bring your own transport.
