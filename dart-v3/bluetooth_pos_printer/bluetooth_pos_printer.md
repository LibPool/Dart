# bluetooth_pos_printer

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A robust Flutter plugin for communicating with Bluetooth thermal (POS) printers. Supports Bluetooth Classic (SPP) on Android and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) on iOS.
