# flutter_touchless

**Tag**: gestures, touchless, camera, accessibility, ui, mobile, tooling

## 简介

A hands-free touchless interaction framework for Flutter with air swipes, virtual air cursor, dwell-click activation, head tracking, and proximity wave gestures.
