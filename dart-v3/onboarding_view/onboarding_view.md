# onboarding_view

**Tag**: mobile, ui, networking

## 简介

A modern, highly customizable onboarding for Flutter — glassmorphism, Apple liquid glass, minimal & material styles, rich page/indicator animations, network/GIF/asset media, and zero-dependency local persistence.
