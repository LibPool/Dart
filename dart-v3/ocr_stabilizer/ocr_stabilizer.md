# ocr_stabilizer

**Tag**: ocr, overlay, tracking, slam, flutter, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Real-time OCR overlay stabilization engine — drift correction, spatial indexing, block tracking, CJK-aware paragraph grouping. Pure Dart; Flutter-ready.
