# condition_aggregator_digitraffic

**Tag**: aggregator, advisory, digitraffic, finland, winter-driving

## 简介

Fintraffic Digitraffic traffic-announcements adapter for the condition_aggregator interface. Maps Finnish traffic announcements to source-neutral Advisory events. Pure Dart.
