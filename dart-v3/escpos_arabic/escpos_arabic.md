# escpos_arabic

**Tag**: escpos, printer, thermal-printer, arabic, receipt, web

## 简介

ESC/POS receipt bytes in pure Dart with first-class Arabic: shaping, bidi, CP864/CP1256, raster images, barcodes and QR codes for thermal printers.
