# ott_stream_kit

**Tag**: ott, video, streaming, player, flutter, mobile

## 简介

A complete Flutter package for OTT video streaming platforms. Provides customizable video player, thumbnail cards, and content rows for apps like Netflix, Hotstar, and VIX Digital OTT.
