# fhir_mapping

**Tag**: fhir, hl7, healthcare, structuremap, interoperability, ui, tooling

## 简介

FHIR Mapping Language parser and StructureMap transform engine for every FHIR version, over the fhir_node reflection contract and a per-version builder model.
