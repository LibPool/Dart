# video_thumbnail_fx

**Tag**: video, thumbnail, image, media, flutter-plugin, web, mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin that creates image thumbnails from local video files or remote video URLs on Android and iOS, with JPEG, PNG, and WebP output.
