# pmtiles_provider

**Tag**: pmtiles, flutter-map, maps, offline, tiles, web, mobile, networking, state-management

## 简介

flutter_map TileProvider for raster PMTiles v3 archives from a file, bytes, an asset or HTTP range requests, with LRU caching, overzoom and cancellation.
