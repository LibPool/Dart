# vrlizate_scene

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Optional flutter_scene (Flutter GPU) adapter for the vrlizate VR engine. Stereo scene rendering, head-tracked eye rigs, and gaze raycasting with dwell selection over real 3D scenes.
