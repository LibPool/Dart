# webmasterid_flutter

**Tag**: web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

WebmasterID for Flutter on iOS and Android — consent-first analytics events, Apple-signed purchase evidence on iOS, bridged to the native WebmasterID SDKs through one native client per platform.
