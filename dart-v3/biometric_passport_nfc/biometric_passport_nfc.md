# biometric_passport_nfc

**Tag**: nfc, passport, mrz, icao, identity, mobile

## 简介

Read ICAO 9303 biometric passports over NFC (PACE/BAC, DG1 identity, DG2 photo) on Android and iOS, with automatic recovery from dropped connections.
