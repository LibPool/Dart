**Tag**: searchable-dropdown, flutter-custom-dropdown, search-field-dropdown, search-dropdown, drop-down, mobile

## 简介

A highly customizable search field dropdown for Flutter supporting single and multi-selection, async search, keyboard navigation, and custom styling.
