# mp_flutter_wechat

**Tag**: wechat, miniprogram, flutter-web, cross-platform, web, mobile

## 简介

WeChat Mini Program APIs (login, payment, and more) for Flutter apps compiled with flutter_miniprogram; throws clear errors on other platforms.
