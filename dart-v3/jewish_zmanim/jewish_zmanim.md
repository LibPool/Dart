# jewish_zmanim

**Tag**: jewish, zmanim, shabbat, sunrise, hebrew-calendar

## 简介

Halachic times (zmanim) per city: sunrise, sunset, nightfall, candle lighting and the Shabbat and Yom Tov window, in the city's time zone.
