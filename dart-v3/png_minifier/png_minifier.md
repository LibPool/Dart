# png_minifier

**Tag**: png, image, compression, optimization, quantization, web, tooling

## 简介

Minify PNG images in pure Dart: many optimisation strategies compete and the smallest result that meets the quality target wins. Lossless by default, bounded-error palette quantisation on request; deterministic on the VM, JS and WebAssembly; with presets and a CLI.
