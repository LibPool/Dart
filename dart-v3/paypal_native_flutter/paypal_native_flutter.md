# paypal_native_flutter

**Tag**: web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Native PayPal card payments and PayPal web checkout for Flutter, wrapping the official paypal-android and paypal-ios SDKs. Server-created Orders v2; your client secret never touches the app.
