# c2pa_audio

**Tag**: c2pa, provenance, audio, ffi, content-credentials

## 简介

Dart FFI bindings to sign and verify C2PA (Content Credentials) in WAV and MP3 audio — ES256/CBOR/JUMBF/COSE, no c2pa-rs. Interoperable with c2pa-rs.
