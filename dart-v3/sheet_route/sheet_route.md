# sheet_route

**Tag**: bottom-sheet, modal, navigation, cupertino, widget, mobile, ui

## 简介

Modal bottom sheet routes for Flutter: Material, bar and iOS-style sheets with scroll-aware drag to dismiss, snap points, go_router Page support and a previous-route scale.
