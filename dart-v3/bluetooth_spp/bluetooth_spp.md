# bluetooth_spp

**Tag**: bluetooth, serial, rfcomm, obd2, android, mobile

## 简介

Bluetooth Classic serial (RFCOMM/SPP) for Android. Discovery, bonding, multiple connections, backpressured writes and typed errors.
