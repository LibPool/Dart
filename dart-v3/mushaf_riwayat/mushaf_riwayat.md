# mushaf_riwayat

**Tag**: flutter, quran, arabic, fonts, qcf, mobile, state-management, tooling

## 简介

Hafs and Warsh Quran rendering with clickable ayahs, fixed mushaf pages, bundled Quran data, and online MP3Quran/EveryAyah audio URL providers.
