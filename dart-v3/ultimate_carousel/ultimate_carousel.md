# ultimate_carousel

**Tag**: carousel, slider, animation, transition, page-indicator, ui

## 简介

Every carousel transition in one package: cube, coverflow, 3D ring, wheel, stack, deck, flip, fan, parallax, reveal, with autoplay, looping and indicators.
