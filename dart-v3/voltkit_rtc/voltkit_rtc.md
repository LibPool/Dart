# voltkit_rtc

**Tag**: webrtc, video, live-streaming, chat, realtime, web, mobile

## 简介

Flutter SDK for VoltKit RTC: realtime audio and video rooms, in-room and standalone chat, and live broadcast publishing over WHIP.
