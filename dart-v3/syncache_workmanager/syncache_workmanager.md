# syncache_workmanager

**Tag**: cache, offline-first, background, workmanager

## 简介

Background flush for Syncache outbox mutations. Wires workmanager's periodic and one-off background tasks to the Syncache mutation outbox so offline writes sync even when the app process has been killed.
