# mcumgr_dart

**Tag**: mobile, tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart client for the SMP / MCUmgr protocol (OS, Image/DFU and Filesystem groups) on Zephyr and MCUboot devices. Bring your own transport; no Flutter.
