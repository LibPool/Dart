# video_thumb_kit

**Tag**: video, thumbnail, flutter-plugin, cross-platform, image-processing, mobile

## 简介

Fast, cross-platform Flutter plugin for generating video thumbnails from files, URLs, and byte arrays, with custom size, quality, format, and timing.
