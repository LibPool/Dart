# flutter_voice_hub

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A comprehensive Flutter plugin providing unified Text-to-Speech (TTS) and  Speech-to-Text (STT) capabilities with rich configuration, real-time  transcription, voice selection, and full lifecycle management for Android and iOS.
