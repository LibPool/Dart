# flutter_wilayah_indonesia

**Tag**: indonesia, address, wilayah, mobile, ui, serialization

## 简介

Cascading Indonesian regions (Prov/Kab/Kec/Kel) with offline JSON, caching, and ready-to-use WilayahPicker widget.
