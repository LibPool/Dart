# a11yship

**Tag**: accessibility, a11y, testing, wcag, semantics, mobile

## 简介

Accessibility checks for Flutter apps (WCAG 2.2, EN 301 549, European Accessibility Act). Finds unlabeled buttons, low contrast, small touch targets and 200% text-size overflows in your tests and points to the line of code to fix.
