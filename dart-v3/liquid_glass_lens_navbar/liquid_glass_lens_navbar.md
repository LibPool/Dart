# liquid_glass_lens_navbar

**Tag**: liquid-glass, bottom-navigation, tab-bar, glassmorphism, ios, mobile, ui

## 简介

An iOS 26 Liquid Glass bottom navigation bar for Flutter: frosted glass, a refracting liquid lens selection, hide on scroll, and adaptive quality.
