# flexi_qr_kit

**Tag**: qr, qrcode, qr-generator, qr-scanner, barcode, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Customizable QR code generator and scanner for Flutter: per-corner module radii, smart corners, styled finders, logos, validation and PNG export.
