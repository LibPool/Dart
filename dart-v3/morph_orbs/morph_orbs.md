# morph_orbs

**Tag**: animation, ai, loading-indicator, chat, ui, state-management

## 简介

Dotted 3D activity orbs for AI chat UIs. Seven lifecycle states, seventeen geometries, and smooth dot-for-dot morphs between any of them. Pure Canvas, no shaders.
