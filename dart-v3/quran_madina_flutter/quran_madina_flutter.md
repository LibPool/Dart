# quran_madina_flutter

**Tag**: quran, arabic, webview, typography, web, mobile

## 简介

Render Quran pages like the printed Madina Mushaf in Flutter: real, selectable text with letters stretched like the print, no images. A WebView wrapper around quran-madina-html.
