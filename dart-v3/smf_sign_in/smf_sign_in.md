# smf_sign_in

**Tag**: flutter, template, feature, sign-in, mobile, state-management

## 简介

The SMF module of the sign-in screens of the Flutter app that SMF generates: sign-in with email or other ways, sign-up, password reset and an account screen, with BLoC or Riverpod.
