# butter_toast

**Tag**: toast, notification, snackbar, animation, ui, mobile

## 简介

Smooth, buttery toasts for Flutter: pill and Sonner-style card toasts, 7 positions with their own motion, promise toasts, swipe to dismiss, no context needed.
