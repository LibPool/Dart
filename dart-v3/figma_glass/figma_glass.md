# figma_glass

**Tag**: glass, glassmorphism, figma, shader, blur, mobile

## 简介

Figma's Glass effect for Flutter: real refraction, dispersion and frost, with every Glass panel setting (light, refraction, depth, dispersion, frost, splay).
