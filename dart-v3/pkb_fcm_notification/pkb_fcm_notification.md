# pkb_fcm_notification

**Tag**: push-notifications, firebase-messaging, fcm, notifications, mobile

## 简介

Push notifications (FCM) for Flutter apps in three calls: permission prompt, device registration, foreground display, tap handling and inbox. Android and iOS.
