# ai_video_editor

**Tag**: video-editor, video, ffmpeg, ai, tiktok, mobile

## 简介

CapCut-style video editor for Flutter with free on-device AI: voice AI Edit, AI Shorts, captions, noise cancellation, cut-out, transitions and FFmpeg export.
