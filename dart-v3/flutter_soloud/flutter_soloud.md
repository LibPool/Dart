# flutter_soloud

**Tag**: audio, player, games, mobile

## 简介

A low-level audio plugin for Flutter, mainly meant for games and immersive apps. Based on the SoLoud (C++) audio engine.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/alnitak/flutter_soloud
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/flutter_soloud
