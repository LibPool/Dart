# universal_document_viewer

**Tag**: pdf, docx, xlsx, document-viewer, reader, mobile

## 简介

Open and read PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), CSV, PowerPoint (.pptx) and text documents in Flutter, with its own document engines.
