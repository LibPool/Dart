# beej

**Tag**: web, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Plants a new Flutter app — material_ui, Riverpod, go_router, en/ne localization, theming, fastlane and the store-screenshot harness, with Appwrite, sqflite and the rest as explicit choices.
