# bbb_meeting_sdk

**Tag**: bigbluebutton, video-conferencing, webrtc, meeting, web, mobile

## 简介

BigBlueButton meeting SDK for Flutter. Pass a join URL, the meeting opens full-screen in the native SDK, and you get the leave reason back.
