# recharge_card_ocr

**Tag**: ocr, camera, text-recognition, scanner, mlkit

## 简介

Read recharge-card, voucher, meter and IMEI digit codes from photos: EXIF fix, zoned crops, OCR digit clean-up, checksums and confidence gating.
