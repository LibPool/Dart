# homeguild_voice_kit

**Tag**: voice, stt, tts, ai, speech, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Voice conversation orchestration for Flutter. Pluggable STT/TTS providers with a complete state machine, sentence buffering, and ready-made widgets.
