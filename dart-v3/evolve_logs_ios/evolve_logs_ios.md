# evolve_logs_ios

**Tag**: web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

iOS implementation of the evolve_logs federated plugin. Forwards the platform channel to the native e-volv Observer iOS client (EvolveLogs), which owns the disk queue, retry and flush — including Launch flags.
