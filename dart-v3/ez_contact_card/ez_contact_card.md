# ez_contact_card

**Tag**: flutter, ez-flutter, contact-card, ui, widgets, mobile

## 简介

A defensive, accessible, and customizable Flutter contact card widget with Material 3 design enforcement, automatic avatar generation, and drop-in ListTile compatibility.
