# ar_quick_look

**Tag**: ar, usdz, quicklook, augmented-reality, ios, mobile, ui

## 简介

Show a USDZ or Reality file in Apple's AR Quick Look, placed in the room through the camera. iOS, with the system viewer rather than a rebuilt one.
