# dart_sentencepiece_tokenizer

**Tag**: nlp, sentencepiece, tokenizer, machine-learning, llm, serialization

## 简介

A lightweight pure Dart SentencePiece tokenizer supporting BPE (Gemma), Unigram (Llama), and Hugging Face tokenizer.json pipelines.
