# snappy_toast

**Tag**: mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

A lightweight, snappy toast and snackbar manager for Flutter. Show beautiful toast messages or stylish snackbars — without needing `BuildContext`, `Navigator`, or `Stack` setup.
