# turkish_kit_flutter

**Tag**: turkish, turkey, money, currency, form, mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter widgets for turkish_kit. A Turkish money field (1.234,56 ₺) with a steady cursor, controller and input formatters for Turkish IDs, IBAN and phone numbers.
