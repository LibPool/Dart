# liquid_glass_floating_tab_bar

**Tag**: navigation, bottom-navigation-bar, glassmorphism, ios, animation, mobile, ui

## 简介

An iOS 26 "Liquid Glass" floating tab bar for Flutter. It has a frosted blur, shrinks on scroll, and has a draggable lens indicator that springs between tabs. Pure Dart, with RTL support.
