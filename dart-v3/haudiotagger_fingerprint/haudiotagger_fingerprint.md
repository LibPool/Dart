# haudiotagger_fingerprint

**Tag**: audio, fingerprint, duplicate-detection, audio-identification, mobile

## 简介

Perceptual audio fingerprinting for Flutter. Identify and compare recordings by content - duplicate detection, library cleanup, song matching. Powered by Rust.
