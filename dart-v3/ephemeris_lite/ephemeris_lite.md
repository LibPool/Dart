# ephemeris_lite

**Tag**: astronomy, ephemeris, chinese-calendar, lunar-calendar, eclipses, tooling

## 简介

Pure Dart astronomy and calendar library with planetary and lunar positions, solar terms, moon phases, eclipses, Chinese calendars and solar time.
