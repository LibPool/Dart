# mrzscanner_flutter

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

MRZ Scanner reads all MRZ on traveling documents following the ICAO 9303 Specification on Passports (2 – lines); MRV-B Visas (2 - lines); and ID cards (3 – lines).
