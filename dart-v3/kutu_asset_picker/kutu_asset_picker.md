# kutu_asset_picker

**Tag**: picker, gallery, crop, image-picker, video, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

A themeable Flutter gallery picker with device-side crop for photos and video: per-asset aspect ratios, trim, cover frames, and a designed limited-access state.
