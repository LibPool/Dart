# fcm_identity

**Tag**: push-notifications, firebase, fcm, notifications, onesignal

## 简介

OneSignal-style push notifications on top of Firebase Cloud Messaging. login/logout users, sync device tokens to your own backend, handle taps and foreground notifications with a few lines of code.
