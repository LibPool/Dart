# flutter_ludo

**Tag**: ludo, game, board-game, bot, multiplayer, web, mobile

## 简介

A complete Ludo game for Flutter: rules engine, animated board and dice, smart bots (easy/medium/hard), 2v2 teams, configurable rules, and save/restore.
