# openskp

**Tag**: sketchup, parser, converter, cad, gltf

## 简介

A pure Dart OpenSKP parser, writer, and converter - create, edit, parse, and convert SketchUp (.skp) files to GLB, OBJ, STL, PLY, DXF, and IFC4.
