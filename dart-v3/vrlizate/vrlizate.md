# vrlizate

**Tag**: vr, virtual-reality, three-d, game-engine, flutter, mobile, ui

## 简介

VR 3D engine for Flutter. Scene graph, mesh rendering, lighting, stereoscopic projection, head tracking, interaction, animation, and effects.
