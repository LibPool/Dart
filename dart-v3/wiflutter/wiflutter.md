# wiflutter

**Tag**: wifi, iot, mobile

## 简介

Control Wi-Fi in Flutter like a boss.
Take the reins of connectivity for Android and iOS with swagger –
connect, disconnect, and snag SSIDs effortlessly.
Your Wi-Fi, your rules – boss mode activated!
