# zema_forms

**Tag**: flutter, forms, validation, schema, zema, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Flutter form widgets backed by Zema schemas. Surgical per-field rebuilds, isTouched UX, auto-focus on first error, and a form-level error banner.
