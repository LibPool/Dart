# libtokensv

**Tag**: tooling

## 简介

BSV token scripts for libspiffy wallets: PushDrop, the token format BRC-100 overlays use, and the TSL1 lineage tokens (TSL1_LT_FT, TSL1_LT_NFT and the fuelled family TSL1_LT_FNFT, with its Voucher and Note kinds) and their libspiffy plugins. Also a client for BSV overlay services (SLAP and SHIP lookup, submit).
