# flutter3d

**Tag**: graphics, game-engine, rendering, gpu, flutter-gpu, mobile

## 简介

A 3D engine on Flutter GPU: scene graph, glTF/OBJ/.f3d loading, PBR, shadows, bloom, skinning.
