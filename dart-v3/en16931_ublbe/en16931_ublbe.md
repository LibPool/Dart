# en16931_ublbe

**Tag**: einvoicing, en16931, ubl, invoice, vat

## 简介

The UBL.BE profile of the European electronic invoice: the identifier it is claimed under, the rules Belgium adds to EN 16931, and the Belgian terms UBL carries beyond it.
