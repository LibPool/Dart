# fold_transition

**Tag**: foldable, animation, hinge, android, mobile, ui

## 简介

Fold transitions for Android foldables. Tracks a continuous hinge angle when the sensor allows it, and plays a fixed-duration animation on quantized hardware such as Galaxy Z Fold.
