# device_check_plus

**Tag**: ios, devicecheck, attestation, fraud-prevention, mobile, testing

## 简介

A minimal Flutter wrapper around Apple's DeviceCheck framework (DCDevice.generateToken). Plain Method Channel — no FFI, no transitive pod dependencies. Maintained alternative to the abandoned `device_check` package.
