# clinical_calc

**Tag**: medical, health, pharmacy, calculator, formula, tooling

## 简介

Validated clinical formulas for pharmacy and medical apps - eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021, MDRD, Cockcroft-Gault, Schwartz), BSA, dosing arithmetic, QTc and risk scores.
