# bd_nid_ocr

**Tag**: state-management

## 简介

Framework-agnostic Bangladesh NID OCR: image cropping, ML Kit text/barcode recognition, and regex/MRZ field extraction. No GetX/Riverpod/Bloc/Provider dependency.
