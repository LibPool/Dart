# sloshgen

**Tag**: animation, liquid, codegen, cli, simulation, ui, tooling

## 简介

Offline Dart generator for baked 2D liquid animations, with waves, sticky wall droplets, shaking, split and merge effects, fixed contours, and GIF export.
