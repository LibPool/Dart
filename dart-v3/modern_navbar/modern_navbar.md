# modern_navbar

**Tag**: widget, navigation, bottom-nav, ui, lottie, web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

A highly customizable modern bottom navigation bar for Flutter with pill-shaped design, detached action button, Lottie animation support, and dark/light theme. Works on mobile, web, and desktop.
