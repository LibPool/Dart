# bolivia_plate

**Tag**: ui

## 简介

Bolivia's licence plates for the plate_core library — the blue-on-white `1234ABC` plate with its department box, the diplomatic and consular `12-CD-34` plates and the Mercosur `AB 12345` plate, with their themes, alphabets and advisory validators.
