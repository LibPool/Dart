# pairlink

**Tag**: pairing, lan, tcp, hmac, mdns, networking

## 简介

Local-network pairing between two phones over TCP: an out-of-band secret, a nonce-bound HMAC handshake, replay rejection, heartbeats and takeover. Pure Dart.
