# uabankpay

**Tag**: payments, banking, open-banking, fintech, url-helper, mobile

## 简介

A Flutter-compatible Dart library for generating and parsing Ukrainian direct (IBAN to IBAN) payment links based on the NBU QR specification (version 3). Dart port of @sirkadirov/uabankpay.js
