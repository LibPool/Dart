# iris_method_channel

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

iris_method_channel is a method channel that communicate between C/C++(iris) and dart, which is used by Agora Flutter SDKs.

- 主页: https://www.agora.io
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/AgoraIO-Extensions/iris_method_channel_flutter/tree/main
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/iris_method_channel
