# reaxdb_flutter

**Tag**: database, nosql, offline-first, storage, encryption, mobile, ui, security, tooling

## 简介

Flutter companion for ReaxDB. Opens databases in the app support directory, keeps the encryption key in the Keychain or Keystore, and turns queries into streams for StreamBuilder.
