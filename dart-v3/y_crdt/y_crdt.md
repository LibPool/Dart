# y_crdt

**Tag**: wasm, wit, crdt, offline, peer-to-peer, web, networking

## 简介

y.js (https://github.com/yjs/yjs) Dart port, a CRDT implementation. CRDTs allow for local, offline editing and synchronization of shared data.
