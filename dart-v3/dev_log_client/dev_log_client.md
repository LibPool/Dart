# dev_log_client

**Tag**: logging, debug, developer-tools, dio, flutter, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Flutter/Dart client for dev_log_viewer. Forwards logs to the local viewer with a Dio interceptor, AppLog shortcuts, and raw log sender.
