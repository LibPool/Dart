# fhir_validation

**Tag**: fhir, hl7, healthcare, validation, interoperability

## 简介

FHIR resource validation against StructureDefinitions (structure, cardinality, bindings, extensions, invariants) for every FHIR version, over the fhir_node reflection contract.
