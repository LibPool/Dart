# open_blue_plus

**Tag**: bluetooth, ble, bluetooth-low-energy, iot, beacon, web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

Free, open-source (MIT) Bluetooth Low Energy plugin for Flutter on Android, iOS, macOS, Web, Linux and Windows. Free for commercial use. Scan, connect, read, write, notify.
