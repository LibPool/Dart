# just_deflate

**Tag**: compression, deflate, zlib, gzip, web

## 简介

Pure Dart DEFLATE (RFC 1951) compression and decompression with the zlib (RFC 1950) and gzip (RFC 1952) formats. No dependencies; runs on native, web and Wasm.
