# flutter_edge_ai_embeddings

**Tag**: embeddings, gemma, litert, rag, on-device, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Embedding tokenizers for flutter_edge_ai: Gemma SentencePiece and BERT WordPiece, as a registrable provider. The pipeline they feed lives in flutter_edge_ai.
