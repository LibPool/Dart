# quickauth_flutter

**Tag**: authentication, otp, login, sms, whatsapp, mobile, ui, security

## 简介

Flutter phone authentication SDK — passwordless OTP login over SMS & WhatsApp, OneTap silent re-auth, SMS autofill, and DPDP/GDPR consent.
