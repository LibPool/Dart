# pakdatakit

**Tag**: pakistan, validation, iban, cnic, qr

## 简介

Pakistani data utilities: CNIC, phone, IBAN, postal codes, addresses, Hijri dates, holidays, amounts in words and Raast QR codes. Pure Dart, all platforms.
