# glint_audio_pure

**Tag**: audio, codec, mp3, encoder, web, tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart MPEG-1 Layer III (MP3) encoder and decoder — no native code, no FFI, runs on every platform including the web. Sibling of glint_audio.
