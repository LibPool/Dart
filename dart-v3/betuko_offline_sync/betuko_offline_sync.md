# betuko_offline_sync

**Tag**: offline-first, sync, hive, local-storage, background-sync, mobile

## 简介

Ultra-simple offline-first Flutter package. Just get(), save(), and syncAll(). Automatic local storage with Hive, manual sync when you want.
