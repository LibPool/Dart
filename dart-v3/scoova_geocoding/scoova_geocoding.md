# scoova_geocoding

**Tag**: geocoding, places, location, web, tooling

## 简介

Geocoding geocoding client for api.scoo-va.info/api/v1/geocoding — forward search, autocomplete, reverse, place lookup, structured search, and synchronous batch (up to 100 mixed forward/reverse queries per request).
