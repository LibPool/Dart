# flutter_swipe_deck

**Tag**: cards, swipe, tinder, pagination, ui, mobile

## 简介

A customizable Tinder-style swipeable card deck for Flutter, with built-in pagination, controller-driven swipes, undo, drag overlays and four-way directions.
