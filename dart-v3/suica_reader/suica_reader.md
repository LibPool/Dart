# suica_reader

**Tag**: nfc, felica, suica, pasmo, transit, mobile, ui

## 简介

Read Suica, PASMO, and compatible FeliCa transit card data on NFC-enabled Android and iOS devices — balance, recent trip history, and raw card identity.
