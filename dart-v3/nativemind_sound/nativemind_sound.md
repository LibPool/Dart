# nativemind_sound

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Sound-healing widgets of NativeMind from handmade code: Pavlo Matvienko's play button and preset selector (2021), Anton Dodonov's iScillator selectors, search screen, pages and navigation (2021–22), editions from configuration (NmConfigs of nativemind_core) and the NmSoundEngine port. Pure Flutter, no plugins.
