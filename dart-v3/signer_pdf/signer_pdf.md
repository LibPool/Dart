# signer_pdf

**Tag**: pdf, digital-signature, certificate, ffi, security, mobile, desktop

## 简介

100% local PDF signing: validate .p12 certificates, validate signed PDFs and sign PDFs with a QR stamp. Android, Windows and Linux.
