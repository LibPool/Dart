# flutter3d_voxel

**Tag**: game-development, voxel, game-engine, procedural-generation, mobile, state-management

## 简介

A world of blocks: chunks, a seeded terrain, edits kept as deltas, greedy meshes to draw, greedy boxes to collide with and the navigation mesh baked again where an edit landed.
