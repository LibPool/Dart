# easy_flutter_ads

**Tag**: ads, admob, monetization, app-open, consent, mobile

## 简介

Production-grade AdMob wrapper for Flutter: preloading with TTL and backoff, AdMob-policy-safe App Open ads, UMP consent, adaptive banners and impression-level revenue events.
