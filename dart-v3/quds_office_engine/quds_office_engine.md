# quds_office_engine

**Tag**: office, docx, xlsx, pptx, pdf, mobile, ui

## 简介

Pure Dart Office + PDF engine: DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, formulas, BiDi/RTL, PdfFile, pdf_widgets, 100+ report templates, and PDF 1.7 — no Flutter.
