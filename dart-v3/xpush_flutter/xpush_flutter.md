# xpush_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, networking

## 简介

Cross-platform Flutter plugin for xNotify/XPush push notifications, in-app notifications, notification center, unread counts, deep linking, campaign tracking, event tracking, and socket management on Android and iOS.
