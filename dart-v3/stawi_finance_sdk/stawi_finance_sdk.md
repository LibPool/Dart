# stawi_finance_sdk

**Tag**: cryptography, ethereum, fintech, mobile, security, tooling

## 简介

Stawi client SDK: canonical FinancialIntent encoding, intent digests, account derivation, policy hashes, manifest verification, exact money arithmetic and device signing. Pure Dart; no Flutter, no transport.
