# flutter_esptool

**Tag**: esp32, esp8266, serial, iot, embedded, mobile

## 简介

ESP8266/ESP32 serial bootloader toolkit for Flutter and Dart. Typed results, chip discovery, flash workflows, and protocol-safe transport abstractions.
