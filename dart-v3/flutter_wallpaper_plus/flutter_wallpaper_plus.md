# flutter_wallpaper_plus

**Tag**: wallpaper, live-wallpaper, android, video, image, mobile

## 简介

Production-grade Flutter plugin for image, auto-changing image, and video
  (live) wallpapers on Android. Supports assets, files, URLs, caching,
  thumbnails, and structured error handling.
