# gfs_backup

**Tag**: backup, retention, rotation, archive

## 简介

Grandfather-Father-Son backup retention and rotation in pure Dart, with no dependencies: decide which daily generations to keep (7 daily / 4 weekly / 6 monthly), rotate them on any backend you plug in, or keep a rotating local archive.
