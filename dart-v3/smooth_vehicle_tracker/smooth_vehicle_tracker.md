# smooth_vehicle_tracker

**Tag**: maps, geolocation, google-maps-flutter, mobile

## 简介

Dead-reckoning and polyline-based vehicle tracking for Flutter maps (Google Maps): sparse SSE updates, route snapping, smoothed heading.
