# no_nepali_profanity

**Tag**: profanity, moderation, nepali, devanagari, filter

## 简介

Profanity matcher for English, Romanized Nepali and Devanagari Nepali. Catches leetspeak, stretched and spelled-out letters and Nepali postpositions, without flagging real names.
