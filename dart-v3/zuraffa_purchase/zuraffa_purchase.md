# zuraffa_purchase

**Tag**: purchase, clean-architecture, zuraffa

## 简介

Purchase support for the Zuraffa ecosystem: a typed in-app purchase port behind a service, with the StoreKit / Play Billing adapters shipped as federated siblings.
