# uzmaps_core

**Tag**: flutter, maps, design-system, animation, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Foundation of the UzMaps Flutter apps — design tokens, motion engine, strings, geo and formatting, async state and on-device stores. No third-party dependencies.
