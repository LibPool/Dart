# flutter_uptick_ads

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

The Flutter Uptick Plugin enables seamless integration of Uptick offers into your Flutter application. This plugin supports pop-up, inline, and popup_then_inline offers, allowing you to display personalized offers based on specific placements and user data. It is designed to work on both Android and iOS platforms with minimal configuration.
