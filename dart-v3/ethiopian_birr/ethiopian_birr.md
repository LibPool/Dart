# ethiopian_birr

**Tag**: ethiopia, currency, birr, amharic, i18n

## 简介

Format Ethiopian Birr in Amharic and Latin script, including Amharic amount-in-words, Ge'ez numerals, and ETB string parsing. Pure Dart, zero dependencies.
