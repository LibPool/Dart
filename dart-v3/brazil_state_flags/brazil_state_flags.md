# brazil_state_flags

**Tag**: flags, brazil, icons, vector, localization, state-management

## 简介

Bandeiras dos 26 estados brasileiros e do Distrito Federal. Flags of the Brazilian states, as vectors in pure Dart. No assets, no dependencies, sharp at any size.
