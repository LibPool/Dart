# dartrosa_calendars

**Tag**: odk, xforms, calendar, date, data-collection

## 简介

ODK Collect's non-Gregorian date appearances for DartRosa: Ethiopian, Coptic, Islamic, Bikram Sambat, Myanmar, Persian and Buddhist calendars.
