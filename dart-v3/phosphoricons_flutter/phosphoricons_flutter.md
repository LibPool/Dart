# phosphoricons_flutter

**Tag**: icons, phosphor, ui, design, mobile

## 简介

Phosphor Icons for Flutter — 1500+ icons in 6 styles (thin, light, regular, bold, fill, duotone). Dart 3.x compatible. Based on phosphor-icons/core v2.0.8.
