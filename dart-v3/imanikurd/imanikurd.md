# imanikurd

**Tag**: islam, quran, kurdish, prayer-times, qibla

## 简介

Complete Islamic toolkit for the Kurdish community — Quran, 13 Kurdish Tafsirs, Qibla, prayer times, dhikr, hadith, and more.
