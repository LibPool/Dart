# nepali_address_plus

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Comprehensive, zero-dependency Nepal administrative division data and lookup utilities — Zone (legacy), Province, District, Local Level, and Ward — sourced and verified against MoFAGA (Ministry of Federal Affairs and General Administration).
