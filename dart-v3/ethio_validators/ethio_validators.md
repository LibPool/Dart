# ethio_validators

**Tag**: ethiopia, validation, phone, currency, formatting, mobile

## 简介

Validation and formatting utilities for Ethiopian data: mobile phone numbers (Ethio Telecom & Safaricom) and ETB currency. Pure Dart, dependency-free.
