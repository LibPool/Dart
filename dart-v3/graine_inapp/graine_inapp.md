# graine_inapp

**Tag**: ai, chatbot, voice, callkit, customer-support, mobile

## 简介

An AI agent inside your Flutter app: it sees the screen, acts in the app, shows components, talks, and takes calls with CallKit on iOS and a call screen on Android.
