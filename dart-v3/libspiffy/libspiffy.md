# libspiffy

**Tag**: bitcoin, wallet, spv, event-sourcing, actors

## 简介

An actor-based Bitcoin SV SPV wallet library: event-sourced wallets, BEEF payments with merkle-proof validation, ARC broadcast, payment channels and pluggable token scripts.
