# video_cacher

**Tag**: video, download, m3u8, hls, offline, mobile

## 简介

Offline video cache for Flutter - mp4/HLS(m3u8) download with resume, AES-128, pure-Dart remux to mp4, album export. 离线视频缓存：断点续传、纯 Dart 转封装、相册保存。
