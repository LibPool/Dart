# yalla_pay_sudan

**Tag**: payment, payment-gateway, sudan, webview, web, mobile

## 简介

Flutter SDK for YallaPaySudan payment gateway. Supports one-time payments, subscriptions, webhook verification, and in-app WebView checkout.
