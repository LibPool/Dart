# guidester

**Tag**: feedback, testing, qa, bug-reports, overlay, mobile, ui

## 简介

In-app tester feedback for Flutter, self-hosted. Testers tap anywhere, type, and send; comments land on your dashboard with a screenshot, screen name and device context.
