# nepali_date_pro_max

**Tag**: library

## 简介

Production-grade Bikram Sambat ↔ Gregorian converter with a full date-fns-style utility layer. Supports BS 1975–2099, locale-aware formatting (en/ne), calendar grids, fiscal-year helpers, distance formatters, and range conversion.
