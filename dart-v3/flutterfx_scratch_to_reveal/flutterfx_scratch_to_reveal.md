# flutterfx_scratch_to_reveal

**Tag**: animation, ui, widget, gesture, mobile

## 简介

A Flutter widget that reveals hidden content through a scratch gesture, with customizable gradient overlay and completion animation. Part of the FlutterFX collection of animated widgets.
