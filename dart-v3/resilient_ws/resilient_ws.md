# resilient_ws

**Tag**: websocket, sse, realtime, network, reconnect, web, mobile, networking, tooling

## 简介

WebSocket and Server-Sent Events clients that survive bad mobile networks: backoff with jitter, fresh tokens, heartbeats, buffering and resubscribe.
