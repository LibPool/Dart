# vertical_credit_card

**Tag**: credit-card, fintech, vertical-card, card-wallet, wallet, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

A modern, customizable vertical credit/debit card UI widget for Flutter fintech apps with 3D flip, metallic, glassmorphic, and frozen card states.
