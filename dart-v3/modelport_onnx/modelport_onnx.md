# modelport_onnx

**Tag**: ai, onnx, machine-learning, on-device, mobile, desktop, serialization

## 简介

ONNX Runtime adapter for ModelPort. Runs onnx variants of modelport.json bundles on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, and Linux.
