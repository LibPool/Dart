# degree_picker

**Tag**: picker, education, form, dropdown, search, mobile, serialization

## 简介

Searchable degree picker and education qualification dropdown for Flutter forms. 1000+ degrees, diplomas, master and PhD worldwide, offline JSON, bottom sheet or dialog.
