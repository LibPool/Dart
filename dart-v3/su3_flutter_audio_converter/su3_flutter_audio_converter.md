# su3_flutter_audio_converter

**Tag**: audio, ffmpeg, converter, ffi, mp3, mobile

## 简介

Converts audio files (mp3, m4a, wma, wav, aac) fully on the device with a bundled minimal FFmpeg, via dart:ffi.
