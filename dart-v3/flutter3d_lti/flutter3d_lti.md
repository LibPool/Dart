# flutter3d_lti

**Tag**: lti, xapi, education, web, mobile, state-management, tooling

## 简介

LTI 1.3 launch and xAPI reporting: OIDC login, id_token verification against a platform's JWKS, an Assignment and Grade Services client, and an xAPI statement client — no shelf, no jaspr, no Flutter.
