# wixzel_voice_flutter

**Tag**: voice-ai, flutter, ai-agents, animation, mascot, mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter widgets for Wixzel Voice. Draw an agent's animated mascot in your app and make it move and lip-sync with a live realtime call.
