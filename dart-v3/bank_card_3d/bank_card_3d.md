# bank_card_3d

**Tag**: credit-card, payment, animation, bank-card, widget, mobile, ui

## 简介

Visa and Mastercard cards for Flutter in real 3D: photoreal or glass, every tier, a wallet stack, a live entry form and a minting animation. Zero dependencies, all six platforms.
