# hmssdk_flutter

**Tag**: web, mobile

## 简介

Add Real Time Audio & Video calls, Interactive Live Streaming & Recording, Chat, HLS, RTMP,  PiP, CallKit, VoIP, Video conferencing, Stream Player & WebRTC-based communications API
