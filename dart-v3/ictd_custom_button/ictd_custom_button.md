# ictd_custom_button

**Tag**: ui

## 简介

A customizable button widget developed by the Innovation and Connectivity for Transformative Digitalization (ICTD) of the Local Government Unit of San Francisco, Agusan del Sur. Supports multiple styles, and sizes.
