# flutter_beep_plus

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A fork to flutter_beep plugin. A very lite module to play system sounds and beep for flutter apps (no sound files).

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/bimoajif/flutter_beep_plus
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/flutter_beep_plus

## 历史版本号

- 0.0.3 (2024-11-15)
