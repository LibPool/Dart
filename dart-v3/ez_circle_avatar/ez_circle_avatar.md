# ez_circle_avatar

**Tag**: avatar, circle-avatar, initials, user-avatar, ez-flutter, mobile, ui

## 简介

A defensive, accessible, and customizable Flutter avatar widget with automatic initials, deterministic colors, status badges, and tap support.
