# immersive_scaffold

**Tag**: widget, ui, scaffold, app-bar, animation, mobile

## 简介

Edge-to-edge iOS-26-style Flutter scaffold with a progressive top blur, a large-to-floating title crossfade, a safe-area-aware scrollbar, and chat, header, and footer layouts.
