# nerf

**Tag**: testing, tooling

## 简介

A deployment toolkit providing CLI helpers to upload to testflight, appstore, playconsole, (similar to Fastlane)

- 源码仓库: https://github.com/evaisse/dart_nerf
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/nerf
