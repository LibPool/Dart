# esp_service

**Tag**: push-notifications, fcm, firebase-messaging, notifications, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Flutter client of ESP (Email & Push Service): registers the device's FCM token, shows push notifications and reports delivery and taps. Android only for now; iOS is planned.
