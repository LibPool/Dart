# fold_kit

**Tag**: foldable, responsive, layout, iphone-duo, adaptive, mobile

## 简介

Foldables and large screens in Flutter: iPhone Duo and Android fold position, posture, hinge angle, two-pane layouts and iPhone Duo-style vertical bars.
