# awesome_custom_dialog

**Tag**: dialog, widgets, forms, animation, authentication, mobile, ui, security

## 简介

A chainable toolkit for Flutter: dialogs, toasts, snackbars, form fields, switches, progress indicators, PIN/OTP fields, motion, and decorations. Zero dependencies.
