# rag_kit

**Tag**: rag, retrieval, embeddings, llm, nlp, ui, tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart RAG (retrieval-augmented generation): chunking, embeddings, vector search, and context building. Zero dependencies, bring your own embedding model.
