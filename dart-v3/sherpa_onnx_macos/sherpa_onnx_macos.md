# sherpa_onnx_macos

**Tag**: speech-recognition, speech-synthesis, speaker-identification, audio-tagging, voice-activity-detection, desktop

## 简介

Speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speaker recognition using next-gen Kaldi with onnxruntime without Internet connection.
