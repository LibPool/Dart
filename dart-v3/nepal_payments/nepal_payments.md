# nepal_payments

**Tag**: payments, nepal, fonepay, qr, mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter widgets for Nepali payments. A Fonepay QR checkout, a searchable bank-app picker that opens the payer's bank app, NPR formatting, in English and Nepali.
