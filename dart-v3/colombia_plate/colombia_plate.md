# colombia_plate

**Tag**: ui

## 简介

Colombia's licence plates for the plate_core library — the `ABC·123` plate in its private, public-service, official, antique, mototaxi, trailer, tank-truck and diplomatic-staff liveries, the new diplomatic-corps plate and the National Police plate, with their themes, alphabets and advisory validators.
