# liquid_glass_toolkit

**Tag**: glassmorphism, ui, animation, shaders, widget, mobile

## 简介

Configurable liquid-glass surfaces, controls, navigation bars, overlays and spring-driven animations for Flutter, inspired by Apple's Liquid Glass design.
