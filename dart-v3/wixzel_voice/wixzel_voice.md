# wixzel_voice

**Tag**: voice-ai, telephony, flutter, sip, ai-agents, web, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Official Dart SDK for the Wixzel Voice API. Build AI agents that place and answer real phone calls over your own SIP trunk, from Dart and Flutter.
