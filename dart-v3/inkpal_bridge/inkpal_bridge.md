# inkpal_bridge

**Tag**: mcp, ai, claude, copilot, agent, web, mobile

## 简介

Turn your Flutter app into an MCP server. AI editors (Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex, Copilot) inspect, drive, hot-reload, auto-recover it. 80 tools. Zero deps.
