# flutter_ota

**Tag**: bluetooth, ble, ota, iot, esp32, web, mobile, ui, networking

## 简介

Ship firmware without a cable — OTA updates for ESP32-class BLE devices (ESP-IDF/Arduino). MQTT and Wi-Fi HTTP/HTTPS support are planned.
