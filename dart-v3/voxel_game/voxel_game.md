# voxel_game

**Tag**: voxel, game, game-engine, sandbox, flutter-scene, mobile, state-management

## 简介

A voxel sandbox in a few lines: declare blocks, world generation, player and mobs, get a playable world on flutter_scene. The kit over voxel_engine, voxel_scene and sound_recipes.
