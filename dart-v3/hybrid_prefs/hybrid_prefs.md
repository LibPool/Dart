# hybrid_prefs

**Tag**: storage, security, shared-preferences, secure-storage, persistence

## 简介

Type-safe SharedPreferences with synchronous reads, where secure keys (tokens, secrets) live in the Keychain/Keystore and are migrated out of plaintext automatically.
