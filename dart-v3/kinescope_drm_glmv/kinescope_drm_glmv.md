# kinescope_drm_glmv

**Tag**: mobile, security

## 简介

Kinescope Flutter player with DRM authorization support. Sends a drmauthtoken so a Kinescope authorization backend can decide who may play a video. Fork of flutter_kinescope_sdk 0.2.4.
