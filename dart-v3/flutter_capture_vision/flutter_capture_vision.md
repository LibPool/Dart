# flutter_capture_vision

**Tag**: barcode, mrz, document-detection, capture-vision, dynamsoft, mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for Dynamsoft Capture Vision — barcode, MRZ, and document boundary detection from image files or raw buffers on six platforms.
