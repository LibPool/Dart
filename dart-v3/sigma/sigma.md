# sigma

**Tag**: spreadsheet, excel, editor, sheets, web, mobile

## 简介

Sigma's native spreadsheet editor in Flutter apps, through the Sigma iOS and Android SDKs. Formulas, formatting, charts, Vani AI and saving; your server mints the links.
