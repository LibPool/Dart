# litlyx_flutter

**Tag**: analytics, tracking, privacy, gdpr, events, mobile

## 简介

Cookie-free, privacy-friendly analytics for Flutter apps with Litlyx: screen views, custom events, sessions and an offline queue, in pure Dart.
