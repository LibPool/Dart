# dpop_client

**Tag**: dpop, oauth2, jwt, authentication, security, web, tooling

## 简介

DPoP proof JWTs for Dart (RFC 9449) — ES256 key handling, PEM storage, JWK thumbprints, access-token binding and server-supplied nonces.
