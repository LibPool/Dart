# sm_crypto_flutter

**Tag**: cryptography, encryption, sm2, sm3, sm4, web, mobile, security

## 简介

Pure Dart implementation of the Chinese national cryptography standards SM2, SM3 and SM4 (国密算法), API-aligned and interoperable with sm-crypto (JavaScript).
