# adaptive_liquid_glass

**Tag**: liquid-glass, glassmorphism, ios, widget, cupertino, web, mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter Liquid Glass widgets: iOS 26 Liquid Glass buttons, tab bar, sheets, sliders and toggles matched to SwiftUI, with Material 3 fallbacks on Android. One adaptive API.
