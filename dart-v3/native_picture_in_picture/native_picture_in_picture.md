# native_picture_in_picture

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A Flutter plugin for native Picture-in-Picture (PiP) on Android and iOS. Uses ExoPlayer and AVKit for smooth, reliable PiP with full lifecycle events and auto-PiP support.
