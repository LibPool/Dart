# iphone_duo_adapt

**Tag**: foldable, iphone-duo, adaptive, responsive, layout, mobile, testing

## 简介

Make an existing Flutter app iPhone Duo ready. Native hinge and fold bridge, rail and split retrofits, fold-safe layouts, preview, test kit, audit and auto-migration.
