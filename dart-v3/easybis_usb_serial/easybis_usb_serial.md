# easybis_usb_serial

**Tag**: usb, serial, uart, android, mobile

## 简介

Android USB serial plugin for Flutter on usb-serial-for-android: CH34x, CP21xx, FTDI, CDC-ACM and Prolific PL2303 including the newer G/HXN family (e.g. PL2303GC 067b:23a3). Fork of usb_serial.
