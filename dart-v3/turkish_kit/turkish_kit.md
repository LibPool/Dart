# turkish_kit

**Tag**: turkish, turkey, validation, iban, holidays

## 简介

Pure Dart Turkish toolkit. TC kimlik, VKN, IBAN and phone validators, amounts in words, Turkish casing and sorting, 81 provinces with districts, holidays and business days.
