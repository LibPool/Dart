# ecg_paper

**Tag**: ecg, chart, healthcare, visualization, flutter, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Scrollable ECG "paper" widget (25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV) for Flutter. Feed mV samples and sample rate to render clinical-style grids and trace.
