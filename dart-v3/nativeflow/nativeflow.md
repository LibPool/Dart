# nativeflow

**Tag**: background, foreground-service, notifications, live-activities, runtime, mobile, networking

## 简介

OS-compliant background runtime for long-running Flutter apps. Keep your own socket/MQTT/location libraries; get recovery, persistent events and notifications.
