# satellite_observer

**Tag**: satellite, sgp4, tle, pass-prediction, visibility, web, mobile

## 简介

A pure-Dart engine for SGP4 satellite propagation, topocentric look-angles, pass prediction, and naked-eye visibility. No Flutter dependency.
