# app_intents

**Tag**: siri, shortcuts, app-intents, appfunctions, assistant, mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for iOS App Intents and Android AppFunctions integration. Enables Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, and AI agent support.
