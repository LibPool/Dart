# sea_glass_ui_flutter

**Tag**: glass, button, shader, widget, ui, mobile

## 简介

Frosted, softly tumbled sea-glass buttons and surfaces for Flutter. A procedural GPU material with seeded wonky outlines, palettes, and Material You support. A Flutter port of Sea Glass for Compose.
