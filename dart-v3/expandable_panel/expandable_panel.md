# expandable_panel

**Tag**: expandable, accordion, collapsible, widget, accessibility, web, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Expandable and collapsible panels for Flutter with accordion groups, accessibility, keyboard and RTL support, reduced motion and state restoration.
