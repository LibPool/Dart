# app_alerts

**Tag**: web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

App-wide, prioritized alert delivery for Flutter. Polls (or receives pushed) alerts from a configurable backend source, tracks what has already been presented, and shows urgent pop-up alerts above every route — or hands inline alert content to the host app to render in its own style. Pure-Dart transport; runs on mobile, desktop, web, and embedded.
