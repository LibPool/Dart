# ai_voice_orb

**Tag**: ai, voice, audio, visualizer, conversational-ai, ui, state-management

## 简介

Fluid, audio-reactive neural voice orb for Conversational AI, Gemini Live & Siri style voice agents with multi-state animations, OKLab color blending, and GPU shaders.
