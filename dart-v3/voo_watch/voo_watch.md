# voo_watch

**Tag**: flutter, watch, wearable, watchos, wear-os, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Flutter SDK for Apple Watch and Wear OS companion apps. Typed message bridge, haptics, health, complications, and a scaffolding CLI.
