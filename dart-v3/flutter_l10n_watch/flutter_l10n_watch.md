# flutter_l10n_watch

**Tag**: flutter, localization, l10n, i18n, cli, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Translation health checker for Flutter: missing, unused and undefined keys, placeholder and plural mistakes, hardcoded strings and RTL smells (easy_localization, ARB).
