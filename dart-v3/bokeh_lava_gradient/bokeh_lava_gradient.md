# bokeh_lava_gradient

**Tag**: animation, gradient, background, widget, mobile, ui

## 简介

Animated bokeh / lava gradient backgrounds for Flutter — soft colored blobs drifting under a Gaussian blur, with ready-made light & dark presets.
