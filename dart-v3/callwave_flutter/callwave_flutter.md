# callwave_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, tooling

## 简介

Flutter plugin for WhatsApp, Google Meet, and Teams-style VoIP call UX. Incoming/outgoing notifications, accept/decline/timeout, CallKit and Android full-screen intents.
