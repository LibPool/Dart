# local_ai_llama_cpp

**Tag**: library

## 简介

llama.cpp adapter for LocalAI Kit: runs any GGUF model as a LocalLlm (streaming, GBNF-constrained structured output) and as a LocalEmbedding, with all FFI work isolated in worker isolates.
