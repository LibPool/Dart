# koni_codecs

**Tag**: compression, lzma, deflate, cryptography, security

## 简介

Pure Dart compression codecs (deflate, LZMA/LZMA2 — decode and encode) and crypto primitives, usable standalone or inside the koni_archive ecosystem.
