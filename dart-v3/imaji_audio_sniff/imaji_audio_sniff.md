# imaji_audio_sniff

**Tag**: audio, flutter, wav, mp3, flac, mobile

## 简介

Dart & Flutter: find the real audio format (WAV, FLAC, MP3, AIFF, M4A) and exact duration from bytes when a lying extension shows the wrong duration or plays silence.
