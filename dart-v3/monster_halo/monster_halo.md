# monster_halo

**Tag**: vivo, iqoo, led, hardware, android, mobile

## 简介

Drive the iQOO/Vivo Monster Halo LED ring from Flutter — solid colors, marquee, chase, comet, strobe and per-LED control over the vivo_light_service binder.
