# utd_video_effects_kit

**Tag**: video, webrtc, filters, beauty, web, mobile

## 简介

Real-time video filters and beauty effects for Flutter WebRTC apps — LUT color grading, skin retouch, makeup, accessories, and background blur as a drop-in video track processor.
