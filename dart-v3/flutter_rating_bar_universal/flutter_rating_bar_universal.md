# flutter_rating_bar_universal

**Tag**: rating, rating-bar, star-rating, widgets, svg, mobile, ui

## 简介

A universal Flutter rating bar with icon, SVG/PNG asset, and custom widget support — half ratings, drag gestures, animations, and read-only mode.
