# sunmi_utils

**Tag**: sunmi, printer, scanner, pos, barcode, ui

## 简介

Scanner and printer utilities for Sunmi POS devices: barcode scanner stream, and printing text, QR codes, barcodes, tables, and images on the built-in Sunmi printer.
