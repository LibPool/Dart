# flutter_banuba_ar

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for the Banuba augmented reality SDK. Provides camera capture, AR effect loading/switching, and a stream of AR-processed frames (RGBA/BGRA) for use in live streaming, recording, or preview.
