# ua_cryptography

**Tag**: cryptography, ffi, signature, mobile, security

## 简介

Ukrainian e-signatures for Dart and Flutter: sign and verify CAdES, XAdES and ASiC with DSTU 4145 keys from JKS key stores, and DSTU 7564 (Kupyna) hashing, over a native library.
