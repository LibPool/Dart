# chinese_lunar_almanac

**Tag**: chinese-calendar, lunar-calendar, almanac, fengshui, ui

## 简介

A pure Dart Chinese traditional almanac (Huangli) library with GanZhi, solar terms, flying stars, lunar mansions, and Yi/Ji.
