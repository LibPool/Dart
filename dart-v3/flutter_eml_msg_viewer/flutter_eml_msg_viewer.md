# flutter_eml_msg_viewer

**Tag**: web, mobile, ui

## 简介

A Flutter widget and parser for previewing .eml (RFC822/MIME) and .msg (Outlook CFBF/MAPI) email files on Android and iOS with zero external dependencies.
