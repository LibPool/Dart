# solar_iconkit

**Tag**: icons, svg, ui, iconography, widgets, mobile

## 简介

Solar icon kit for Flutter. 1,269 icons in 6 native styles — linear, outline, broken, bold, line-duotone, bold-duotone — behind a single SolarIcon widget.
