# fluid_carousel

**Tag**: carousel, slider, animation, ui, widgets, mobile, desktop, tooling

## 简介

A modern, fluid, and extensible carousel widget for Flutter with zero-rebuild reactive indicators, 3D/parallax transformers, desktop gestures, and sliver support.
