# qvac_flutter

**Tag**: speech-to-text, whisper, on-device-ai, offline, mobile, desktop

## 简介

Unofficial Flutter plugin for QVAC, Tether's local AI. On-device speech to text with Whisper on iOS, Android, macOS, Linux and Windows.
