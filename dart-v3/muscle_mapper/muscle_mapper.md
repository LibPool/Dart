# muscle_mapper

**Tag**: ui, fitness, anatomy, svg, body-map, mobile

## 简介

Flutter widget for interactive human body muscle map. Tap to highlight muscles, show workout heatmaps, and visualize anatomy. Includes male/female SVGs.
