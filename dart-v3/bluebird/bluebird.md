# bluebird

**Tag**: bluetooth, ble, bluetooth-low-energy, web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

A Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) plugin for Flutter — scan, connect, and read/write GATT on Android, iOS, macOS, and Web.
