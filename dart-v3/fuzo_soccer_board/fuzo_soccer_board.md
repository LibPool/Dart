# fuzo_soccer_board

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter widget for a tactical soccer/football board with drag-and-drop player positioning, tactical zones, field orientation (portrait/landscape), and read-only mode. Perfect for lineup and formation editors.
