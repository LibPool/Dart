# cheki

**Tag**: ethiopia, payments, receipt, verification, api, web, tooling

## 简介

Dart client for cheki, the free Ethiopian receipt verifier. Verify CBE, Telebirr, BOA, M-Pesa, Dashen, Awash, Siinqee, eBirr, ZamZam and CBE Birr transfer receipts.
