# graphview_plus

**Tag**: graph, force-directed, visualization, network, canvas, mobile, ui, networking

## 简介

Interactive force-directed graph for Flutter: d3-style physics that settles without jitter, non-overlapping nodes, pan/zoom, drag, hover and an Obsidian-like reveal animation.
