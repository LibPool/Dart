# coinlib_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, state-management

## 简介

A flutter wrapper for coinlib, a straight-forward, modular library for Peercoin and other Satoshi-based UTXO blockchains

## 官网

- 源码仓库: https://github.com/peercoin/coinlib
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/coinlib_flutter
