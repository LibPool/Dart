# flame_flutter3d

**Tag**: game-development, game-engine, graphics, flame, mobile

## 简介

A bridge to the Flame 2D game engine: Flame draws its own layer, flutter3d draws its own, and the two stay reconciled — transforms, lifecycle, physics contacts, input and the actor system.
