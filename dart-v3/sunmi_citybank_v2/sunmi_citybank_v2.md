# sunmi_citybank_v2

**Tag**: mobile, security

## 简介

A professional Flutter plugin for Newland / Sunmi POS & City Bank Smart Terminal payment integration, including Card, QR, Barcode, Refund, PreAuth, Settlement, and Thermal Receipt Printing.
