# system_intents

**Tag**: android, intents, camera, alarm, plugin, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Make a Flutter app the Android handler for camera capture and alarm/timer intents, with typed requests, safe EXTRA_OUTPUT replies and a bundled FileProvider.
