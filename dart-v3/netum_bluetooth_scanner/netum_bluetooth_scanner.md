# netum_bluetooth_scanner

**Tag**: barcode, scanner, bluetooth, ble, spp, mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for NETUM Bluetooth barcode scanners (BLE on Android & iOS, SPP on Android): scan streams, multi-device, commands.
