# timezone_country

**Tag**: timezone, country-code, iso-3166, flag-emoji, i18n, desktop

## 简介

Convert IANA timezones to ISO 3166-1 country codes and back: country names, flag emoji, alpha-2/alpha-3, Windows timezone IDs. Pure Dart, zero dependencies.
