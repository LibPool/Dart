# asteroid_ephemeris_lite

**Tag**: astronomy, ephemeris, asteroids, minor-planets, centaurs, mobile

## 简介

Lightweight long-range asteroid and centaur ephemerides for Dart and Flutter, with heliocentric and geocentric J2000 geometric positions.
