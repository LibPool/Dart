# kat_appinio_video_player

**Tag**: web, mobile, ui, networking, tooling

## 简介

A custom video player that builds on top of the official video_player package by flutter and adds many functionalities. Fork of appinio_video_player_plus (https://github.com/appinioGmbH/flutter_packages), maintained here for a dart:ui_web fix not yet released upstream.
