# kurdish_localization_plus

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Flutter Kurdish Localization supporting both Central Kurdish (Sorani/CKB) and Kurmanji (KRM) dialects with comprehensive Material, Widget, and Cupertino localizations.
