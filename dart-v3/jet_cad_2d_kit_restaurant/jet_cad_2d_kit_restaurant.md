# jet_cad_2d_kit_restaurant

**Tag**: cad, floor-plan, restaurant, web

## 简介

The restaurant kit for jet_cad_2d plans: a library of 69 restaurant symbols (tables, booths, bar, service, commercial kitchen, outdoor and decor), the servable ones as service units.
