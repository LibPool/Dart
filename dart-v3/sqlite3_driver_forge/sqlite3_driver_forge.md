# sqlite3_driver_forge

**Tag**: sqlite, driver, isolate, database, serialization, state-management

## 简介

Background-isolate SQLite driver over package:sqlite3 with serialized access and SQLCipher support. Runs sqlite_query_forge and sqlite_schema_forge statements.
