# loq_flutter

**Tag**: logging, flutter, structured-logging, observability, web, mobile

## 简介

Flutter integration for loq structured logging. Ships a Navigator observer with ambient screen-name context, a lifecycle observer that flushes handlers on pause and detach, and an init helper that wires FlutterError, PlatformDispatcher, and zone-guard error capture with chain-and-restore semantics.
