# onboarding_minimal

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

A highly customizable Flutter package designed to enhance the first-time user experience through engaging onboarding flows. Featuring pre-designed pages, dynamic content support, and extensive customization options for animations, buttons, and indicators, it simplifies the creation of informative onboarding processes with minimal setup.
