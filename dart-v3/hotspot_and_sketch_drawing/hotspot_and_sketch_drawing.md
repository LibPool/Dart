# hotspot_and_sketch_drawing

**Tag**: annotation, drawing, canvas, storybook, voice-notes, mobile

## 简介

Drop-in interactive storybook/image viewer for Flutter: swipeable pages, freeform "hotspot" shapes with numbered badges and long-press voice notes, plain "marker" badges, and freehand sketching.
