# qr_scanner_view

**Tag**: qr-code, barcode, scanner, camera, mobile

## 简介

Live camera QR / barcode scanner Flutter plugin. Native owns camera, preview and detection (AVFoundation, CameraX + ML Kit); only decoded strings cross to Dart.
