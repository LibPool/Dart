# geometry_kit_3d

**Tag**: geometry, math, solid, mesh

## 简介

Immutable 3D solids for Dart — volume, surface area, ray picking, cross-sections, extrusion and revolution of geometry_kit 2D shapes, meshes with OBJ/STL export.
