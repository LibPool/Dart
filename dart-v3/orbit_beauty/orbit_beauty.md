# orbit_beauty

**Tag**: web, mobile

## 简介

Face-aware GPU beauty filters for LiveKit / flutter_webrtc camera tracks — skin smoothing, whitening, glow, face slimming and a landmark-placed makeup pack of lips, eyes, blush, contour and highlight, on the capture path.
