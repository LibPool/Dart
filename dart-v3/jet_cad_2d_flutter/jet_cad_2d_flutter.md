# jet_cad_2d_flutter

**Tag**: cad, drawing, canvas, pdf, mobile

## 简介

Flutter rendering and interaction for the jet_cad_2d engine: a CAD canvas with camera, rulers, selection and snapping, and PDF and PNG export.
