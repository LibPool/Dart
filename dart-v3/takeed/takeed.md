# takeed

**Tag**: push-notifications, notifications, firebase-messaging, onesignal, mobile

## 简介

Push notifications for Flutter apps on Takeed (takeed-iq.com), with direct FCM and APNs delivery. Drop-in replacement for onesignal_flutter v5.
