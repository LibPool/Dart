# rumil_parsers

**Tag**: parser, json, toml, ui, serialization

## 简介

Format parsers built on Rumil: JSON, CSV, XML, TOML, YAML, Proto3, HCL, and CommonMark Markdown, plus typed AST decoders with ObjectAccessor pattern.
