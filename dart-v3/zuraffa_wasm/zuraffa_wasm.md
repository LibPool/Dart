# zuraffa_wasm

**Tag**: wasm, clean-architecture, zuraffa, web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

WebAssembly for the Zuraffa ecosystem: a pure-Dart port over an injected platform channel with federated android, ios, and macos adapters.
