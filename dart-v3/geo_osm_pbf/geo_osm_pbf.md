# geo_osm_pbf

**Tag**: serialization

## 简介

Streaming OpenStreetMap `.osm.pbf` reader and extract downloader in pure Dart. Hand-rolled protobuf decoding with no dependencies, delivering nodes, tagged nodes, ways and relations with their full tags.
