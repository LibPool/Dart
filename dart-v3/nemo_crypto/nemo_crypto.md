# nemo_crypto

**Tag**: encryption, security, cryptography, e2ee, libsodium

## 简介

A pure Dart key hierarchy and authenticated encryption architecture (XChaCha20-Poly1305, Argon2id) for local-first, end-to-end encrypted apps
