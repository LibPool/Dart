# nativemind_sound_generator

**Tag**: mobile, tooling

## 简介

Tone generator plugin of NativeMind: sound_generator 0.0.5 by Mert Güner (MIT) with Anton Dodonov's MULTI generator (Android DSP, 2021–22, harmonics gain 2025) and the iOS AudioKit settings of 2021; implements NmSoundEngine of nativemind_sound.
