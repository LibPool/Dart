# hotlayout_flutter

**Tag**: server-driven-ui, sdui, ui, widget, web, mobile, serialization, tooling

## 简介

Flutter renderer for HotLayout server-driven UI. Draws screens published by the HotLayout builder from a JSON document, a theme and your data.
