# kerabie_helpdesk_flutter_calls

**Tag**: calls, customer-support, helpdesk, webrtc, web, mobile

## 简介

Voice and video calls for the Kerabie help desk in a Flutter app. A call button and a call screen on top of kerabie_helpdesk_flutter.
