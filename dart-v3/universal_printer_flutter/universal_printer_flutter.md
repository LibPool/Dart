# universal_printer_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, networking

## 简介

Discover (Epson/Sunmi/Zebra/Star/SNMP/USB) and print ESC/POS receipts to network, USB, Star and Sunmi/iMin printers. Full on Android; network + Star on iOS.
