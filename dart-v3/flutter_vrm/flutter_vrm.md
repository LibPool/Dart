# flutter_vrm

**Tag**: vrm, avatar, rendering, gltf, mobile

## 简介

VRM 1.0 avatars on flutter_scene (Flutter GPU): humanoid poses, expressions, look-at, and auto blink, from a renderer-independent VRM parser.
