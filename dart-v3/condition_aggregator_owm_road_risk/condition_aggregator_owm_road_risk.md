# condition_aggregator_owm_road_risk

**Tag**: aggregator, advisory, openweathermap, winter-driving

## 简介

OpenWeatherMap Road Risk adapter for the condition_aggregator interface. Maps OWM Road Risk alerts to source-neutral Advisory events. Pure Dart.
