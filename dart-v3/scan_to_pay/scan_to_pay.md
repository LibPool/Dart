# scan_to_pay

**Tag**: ocr, scan, payments, nuban, bank, mobile, ui

## 简介

Reusable Flutter SDK for scan-to-pay: OCR-based account-number capture, multi-frame confirmation, pluggable bank resolver, and themeable UI.
