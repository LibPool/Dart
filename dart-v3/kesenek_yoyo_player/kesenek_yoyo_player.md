# kesenek_yoyo_player

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Kesenek YoYo Video Player is a HLS(.m3u8) video player for flutter. The YoyoPlayer is a video player that allows you to select HLS video streaming by selecting the quality. Updated and maintained version with improved stability.
