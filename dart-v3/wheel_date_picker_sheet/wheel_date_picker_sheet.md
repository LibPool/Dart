# wheel_date_picker_sheet

**Tag**: date-picker, datepicker, time-picker, bottom-sheet, wheel, ui

## 简介

A modern, easy date and time picker shown as a bottom sheet with scrolling day / month / year (and hour / minute) wheels. dd/MM/yyyy preview, future dates, English and Lao labels built in.
