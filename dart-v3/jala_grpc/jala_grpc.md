# jala_grpc

**Tag**: network, debugging, devtools, grpc, networking, testing, tooling

## 简介

gRPC ClientInterceptor for Jala: unary and streaming RPC capture with service/method, messages, status codes and trailers. Capture only — mocking and throttling do not apply to gRPC.
