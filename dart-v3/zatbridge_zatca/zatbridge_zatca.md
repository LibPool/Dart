# zatbridge_zatca

**Tag**: zatca, e-invoicing, offline, flutter, saudi-arabia, mobile

## 简介

ZATCA Phase 1 QR generation and Phase 2 offline signing for simplified-subtype (B2C) documents. Produces compliant QR payloads entirely on-device.
