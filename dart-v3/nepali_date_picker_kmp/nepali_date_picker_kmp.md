# nepali_date_picker_kmp

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Nepali (Bikram Sambat) date picker and BS/AD conversion engine for Flutter, bridging the compiled Kotlin Multiplatform library: Material3 pickers, month grids, formatting, digit localization, events and working-day math.
