# sheetifye

**Tag**: excel, xlsx, csv, spreadsheet, editor, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Flutter spreadsheet engine with XLSX/CSV support, live editing, undo/redo, formula evaluation, clipboard, autofill, and a full persistence lifecycle.
