# flutter_audio_fx

**Tag**: audio, dsp, effects, rust, ffi, mobile

## 简介

Real-time voice effects for Flutter powered by a Rust DSP core: auto-tune, pitch shift, noise suppression, EQ, reverb, de-esser and more.
