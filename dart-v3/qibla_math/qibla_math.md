# qibla_math

**Tag**: qibla, compass, geolocation, magnetometer, geodesy, tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart qibla and compass maths. Great-circle qibla bearing, Vincenty distance, tilt-compensated heading, circular angle helpers and WMM2025 magnetic declination.
