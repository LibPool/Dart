# sound_recipes

**Tag**: audio, sound-effects, synthesizer, procedural-audio, game-development, mobile

## 简介

Game sounds with no audio files: write a recipe, it is synthesised to WAV at start-up and played through flutter_soloud. A stock set by material, and music crossfaded by context.
