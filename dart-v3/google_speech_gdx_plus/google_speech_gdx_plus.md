# google_speech_gdx_plus

**Tag**: speech-to-text, speech-recognition, google-cloud, grpc, streaming, mobile, networking, tooling

## 简介

Community-maintained Flutter client for Google Cloud Speech-to-Text over gRPC, with V1, V1p1beta1, V2, streaming, and endless streaming support.
