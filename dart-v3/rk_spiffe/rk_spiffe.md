# rk_spiffe

**Tag**: spiffe, identity, security, x509, jwt

## 简介

SPIFFE identity in pure Dart. Parses and validates spiffe:// IDs and trust domains, and reads X.509-SVID and JWT-SVID documents. Verifies nothing.
