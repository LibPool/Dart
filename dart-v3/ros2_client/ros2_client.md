# ros2_client

**Tag**: ros, ros2, robotics, rosbridge, websocket, web, mobile, networking, tooling

## 简介

A type-safe, streaming ROS 2 client for Dart and Flutter. Topics, services, actions, parameters and introspection over rosbridge.
