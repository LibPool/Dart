# ficonsax

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

ficonsax is Iconsax, ~1000 icons available in 3 styles (Bold, Bulk and Outline) as a dart/flutter package.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/Mastersam07/fIconsax
