# rtcexpress

**Tag**: webrtc, video-call, voice-call, chat, realtime, web, mobile

## 简介

Add voice calls, video calls and in-app chat to Flutter apps with RTCExpress. Hosted signaling, TURN and SFU. Android supported, iOS experimental.
