# unseal

**Tag**: ebook, epub, mobi, fb2, pdf

## 简介

Pure Dart book parsing: extract metadata, covers, content, styles, fonts and navigation from EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, DOCX, ODT, CBZ/CBR and PDF.
