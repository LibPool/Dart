# mapzone_nav_speed_alert

**Tag**: navigation, speed-alert, mapzone, adas, driving, web, mobile

## 简介

Speed-limit, camera, toll and road-restriction alerts with voice along a given route, for Flutter navigation apps on Android and iOS.
