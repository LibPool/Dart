# uz_kit

**Tag**: uzbekistan, fintech, uzcard, formatters, validation, mobile

## 简介

Complete Flutter & Dart utility kit for Uzbekistan. Detects Uzcard/Humo cards, mobile operators, formats money to Uzbek words, phone numbers, and validates PINFL/Passports.
