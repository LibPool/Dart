# ethio_greg_dual_calendar

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Dual-calendar dates and calendar UIs for Flutter in both the Gregorian and Ethiopian (Amete Mihret) calendars, localized in English, Amharic, and Afaan Oromoo.
