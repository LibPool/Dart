# haptic_swipe_slider

**Tag**: slider, swipe-to-confirm, slide-action, haptics, ui, state-management

## 简介

A modern, highly customizable slide-to-confirm pill slider with tactile haptic feedback, spring physics, shimmer trail, and async loading states.
