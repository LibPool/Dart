# zuraffa_messaging

**Tag**: messaging, notifications, clean-architecture, zuraffa

## 简介

Interface-based messaging for the Zuraffa ecosystem: pluggable transports behind one channel-routed facade, with send history, read receipts, and the versioned platform-channel contract the federated native adapters serve.
