# noaa_nws_adapter

**Tag**: weather, noaa, nws, winter-driving, alerts, web, serialization, tooling

## 简介

NOAA / NWS direct-consume wrapper — smallest-slice GeoJSON client for api.weather.gov active winter alerts by point. Pure Dart.
