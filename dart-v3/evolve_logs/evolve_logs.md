# evolve_logs

**Tag**: web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

e-volv Observer and Launch SDK for Flutter. A thin Dart facade over the native Android and iOS Observer clients: the Dart side captures errors, spans and logs and forwards them over the platform channel; the native client owns the disk queue, the retry schedule and the lifecycle-driven flush. Launch flags read synchronously from a native snapshot.
