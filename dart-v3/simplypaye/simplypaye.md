# simplypaye

**Tag**: web, mobile, networking

## 简介

SimplyPaye Mobile Money SDK for Flutter and Dart — Orange Money, Airtel Money, M-Pesa via https://api-simply-pay.net. Merchant signup at marchand-simplypaye.store.
