# flutter_ios_configurator

**Tag**: mobile, desktop

## 简介

Configure iOS capabilities for Flutter projects without Xcode. Works from Windows, macOS, and Linux. Firebase, Push Notifications, Background Modes, and Google Maps — configured in seconds.
