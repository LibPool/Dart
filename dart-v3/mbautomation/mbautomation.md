# mbautomation

**Tag**: library

## 简介

MBAutomation plugin for MBurger that lets you present push and in app messages based on the behavior of the user.

## 官网

- 主页: https://web.mburger.cloud/engagement-platform
- 源码仓库: https://github.com/Mumble-SRL/MBAutomation-Flutter
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/mbautomation
