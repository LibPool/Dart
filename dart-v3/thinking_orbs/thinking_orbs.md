# thinking_orbs

**Tag**: mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Dotted thought-orb loading indicators for AI & agent UIs — six tuned states, two sizes, auto dark/light. A Dart/Flutter reimplementation of Jakub Antalik's thinking-orbs.
