# dartway_push_flutter

**Tag**: dartway, push, notifications, flutter, mobile

## 简介

Push notifications for a DartWay app, reached as dw.plugins.push: token registration that follows the session, permission on demand, and opened notifications with their typed payload. Carries no vendor SDK — add dartway_push_firebase or dartway_push_rustore.
