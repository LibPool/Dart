# activeledger

**Tag**: activeledger, blockchain, dlt, post-quantum, cryptography, mobile, security, state-management

## 简介

Activeledger SDK for Dart and Flutter. Onboard identities, sign payloads, and sign and submit transactions with secp256k1 and post-quantum ML-DSA-65 keys.
