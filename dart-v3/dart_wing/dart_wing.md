# dart_wing

**Tag**: payment, wing, cambodia, checkout, web, mobile, ui, security, tooling

## 简介

Wing Bank online payment (Web SDK v2) client for Dart and Flutter. Authorize a payment, open Wing's checkout page, inquire transactions, and decrypt payment callbacks.
