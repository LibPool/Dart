# seatlayer

**Tag**: seat-map, seating-chart, seat-picker, reserved-seating, event-ticketing, mobile

## 简介

Flutter seat map SDK: a native seating chart and seat picker for iOS and Android ticketing apps, with live availability, best available, seat holds, 3D views and secure checkout.
