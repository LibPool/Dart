# j2k

**Tag**: jpeg2000, jp2, image, codec, decoder

## 简介

Pure Dart JPEG 2000 codec: decodes JP2 and J2K files to 8- or 16-bit pixels with alpha, encodes pixel buffers and PGM/PPM, and runs on the VM, dart2js and dart2wasm.
