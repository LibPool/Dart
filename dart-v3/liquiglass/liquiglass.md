# liquiglass

**Tag**: liquid-glass, glassmorphism, shader, blur, ui, mobile

## 简介

Liquid Glass for Flutter, measured against iOS recordings: refraction, dispersion and the iOS material over the live backdrop, every frame, on Impeller. Android; iOS is not a target.
