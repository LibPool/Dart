# infly

**Tag**: deep-links, deferred-deep-link, dynamic-links, universal-links, app-links, mobile

## 简介

Short links, App Links / Universal Links and deferred deep links for Flutter, backed by infl.ly. Router-agnostic, no device fingerprinting.
