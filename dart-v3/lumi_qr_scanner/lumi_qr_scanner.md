# lumi_qr_scanner

**Tag**: mobile, desktop

## 简介

A fast and lightweight Flutter plugin for scanning barcodes and QR codes using the device's camera. Supports multiple barcode formats with CameraX/ML Kit on Android and AVFoundation on iOS/macOS.
