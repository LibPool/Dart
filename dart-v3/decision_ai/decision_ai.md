# decision_ai

**Tag**: ai, machine-learning, onnx, on-device, llm, web, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Typed decisions (jev-style) in Flutter: choice, noul and score answered by on-device or in-browser models of any family, or by any Decision API provider.
