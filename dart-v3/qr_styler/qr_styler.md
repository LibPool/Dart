# qr_styler

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

A highly customizable Flutter QR code widget with support for styled dots, rounded corners, eye design control, horizontal & vertical connections, and center logo embedding. Easily create modern, branded, and scannable QR codes with flexible design options.
