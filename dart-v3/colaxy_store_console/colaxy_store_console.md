# colaxy_store_console

**Tag**: app-store-connect, google-play, reviews, analytics, reporting, web, tooling

## 简介

Unified Dart client for Google Play Console and App Store Connect. Read reviews and reply to them, and pull sales, analytics, vitals and install reports from both stores through one API.
