# e_book_reader

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A powerful E-Book Reader for Flutter apps. Features adjustable text styles, light/dark modes, horizontal/vertical layouts, and scroll tracking. Perfect for a smooth, user-friendly reading experience.
