# flutter_sea_glass

**Tag**: sea-glass, liquid-glass, glassmorphism, shaders, effects, mobile, ui

## 简介

Sea glass and liquid glass for Flutter: tumbled, frosted and colored glass, milky pastel slabs and living shapes, rendered with Impeller.
