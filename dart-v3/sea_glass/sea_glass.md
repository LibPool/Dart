# sea_glass

**Tag**: glass, shader, material-design, ui, widget, mobile

## 简介

Sea glass for Flutter, frosted and softly tumbled, drawn with a real shader. Seeded outlines, frost, a glowing edge and transmitted light for Material 3 apps.
