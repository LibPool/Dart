# flutter_kevy

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

kevy embedded in Flutter over dart:ffi — the real pure-Rust Redis-compatible engine in your app: TTL, hashes/lists/zsets, pub/sub, persistence. The MMKV shape, and everything MMKV lacks.
