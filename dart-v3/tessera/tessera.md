# tessera

**Tag**: pivot-table, olap, aggregation, csv, data-analysis, mobile, ui

## 简介

Analyze, group and aggregate tabular data: a pivot-table engine with expandable row/column hierarchies. Pure Dart; tessera_flutter adds the widgets.
