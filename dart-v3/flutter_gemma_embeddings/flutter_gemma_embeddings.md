# flutter_gemma_embeddings

**Tag**: embeddings, gemma, litert, rag, on-device, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Embedding tokenizers for flutter_gemma: Gemma SentencePiece and BERT WordPiece, as a registrable provider. The pipeline they feed lives in flutter_gemma.
