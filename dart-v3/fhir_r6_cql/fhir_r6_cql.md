# fhir_r6_cql

**Tag**: fhir, hl7, healthcare, cql, interoperability, state-management

## 简介

FHIR R6 binding for the model-independent `cql` engine: the R6 ModelResolver and TerminologyProvider implementations that let the CQL engine execute against FHIR R6 data.
