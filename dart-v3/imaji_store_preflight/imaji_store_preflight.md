# imaji_store_preflight

**Tag**: flutter, ios, android, app-store, ci, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Catch App Store and Google Play rejections in your Flutter project before review — a Dart CLI that lints Info.plist, privacy manifests and AndroidManifest.
