# uzgeocoder

**Tag**: geocoding, uzbekistan, openstreetmap, location, offline, web

## 简介

Offline reverse geocoder for Uzbekistan. Converts latitude and longitude to region (viloyat) and district (tuman) enums, the nearest street and postal code. Pure Dart.
