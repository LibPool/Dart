# impeccable_flutter_lints

**Tag**: lints, design, analyzer, flutter, custom-lint, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Plugin custom_lint que detecta anti-padrões de design em apps Flutter. 34 regras: AI slop (gradient, deepPurple) e quality (Semantics, contrast).
