# custom_nepali_calendar

**Tag**: calendar, date-picker, nepali, bikram-sambat, localization

## 简介

Nepali Bikram Sambat calendar and date picker: single date or range, selectable dates marked in your own colours, a live BS/AD switch, Nepali or English labels. No dependencies.
