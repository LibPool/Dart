# nadeem_font

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A comprehensive collection of high-quality Arabic fonts including Cairo, Tajawal, Noto Sans Arabic, and IBM Plex Sans Arabic, with easy-to-use utility classes for Flutter apps.
