# vkaxlib_flutter

**Tag**: mobile, state-management

## 简介

A flutter wrapper for vkaxlib, a straight-forward, modular library for Vkax and other Satoshi-based UTXO blockchains

## 官网

- 源码仓库: https://github.com/michaelvkax/vkaxlib
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/vkaxlib_flutter

## 历史版本号
