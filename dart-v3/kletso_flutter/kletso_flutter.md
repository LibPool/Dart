# kletso_flutter

**Tag**: ai, chat, chatbot, sdk, generative-ui, mobile, ui, state-management

## 简介

Flutter widgets for Kletso AI chat: launcher, chat, 30 built-in blocks, custom components, theming, notifications, voice sheet and animated avatar.
