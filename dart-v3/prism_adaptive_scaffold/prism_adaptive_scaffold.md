# prism_adaptive_scaffold

**Tag**: adaptive, responsive, scaffold, navigation, layout, mobile, desktop

## 简介

Responsive adaptive scaffolds for Flutter — the maintained replacement for the discontinued flutter_adaptive_scaffold. Navigation rails, split views, and breakpoints that adapt from phone to tablet to desktop out of the box.
