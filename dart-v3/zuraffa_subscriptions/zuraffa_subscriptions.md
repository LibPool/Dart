# zuraffa_subscriptions

**Tag**: subscriptions, in-app-purchase, entitlements, clean-architecture, zuraffa

## 简介

Typed in-app purchases and subscriptions for the Zuraffa ecosystem: a scriptable store port behind a service that maps transactions into derived entitlements.
