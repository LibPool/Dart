# pilotpm_engage

**Tag**: analytics, engagement, messaging, push, mobile, state-management, tooling

## 简介

PilotPM Engage in-app SDK for Dart/Flutter. Event tracking (identify/track/ screen), push-token registration, in-app messages, push open/click reporting, and the Genie voice companion (a floating mic button). Drops in beside an existing analytics provider (e.g. Braze) for dual-write migration.
