# duo_display

**Tag**: foldable, ios, iphone, display-features, hinge, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Foldable iPhone Duo support for Flutter: hinge, device posture and camera cutouts from iOS 27.1 in MediaQuery.displayFeatures, plus a two-pane layout builder.
