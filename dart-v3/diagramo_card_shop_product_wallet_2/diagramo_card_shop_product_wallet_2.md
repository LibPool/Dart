# diagramo_card_shop_product_wallet_2

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

customizable product wallet card widget for e-commerce applications. This widget displays product information including an image, name, price, and controls for adjusting product quantities. It can be easily integrated into any Flutter app.
