# flutter_vision_lens

**Tag**: vision, camera, ai, multimodal, developer-tools, mobile

## 简介

An interactive Apple Visual Intelligence and Google Lens style multimodal camera and image overlay for Flutter with tap-to-inspect, freehand lasso, glowing shaders, and AI connectors.
