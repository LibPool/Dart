# slide_glass_nav_bar

**Tag**: navigation, bottom-navigation, liquid-glass, rtl, glass, ui

## 简介

A floating liquid-glass bottom nav bar with press-and-hold + slide-to-select gestures, an elastic bounce on commit, and full RTL support.
