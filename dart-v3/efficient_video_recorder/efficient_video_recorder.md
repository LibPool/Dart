# efficient_video_recorder

**Tag**: camera, video-recorder, dashcam, camerax, performance, mobile

## 简介

High-efficiency camera video recorder for Flutter. Optimized for low battery usage and zero thermal throttling with CameraX on Android and AVFoundation on iOS.
