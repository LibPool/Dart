# flutter3d_net_webrtc

**Tag**: game-development, networking, multiplayer, webrtc, web, mobile

## 简介

A NetTransport over a real WebRTC data channel — net-01's rollback engine, P2P, with net-02's relay carrying only the SDP/ICE handshake.
