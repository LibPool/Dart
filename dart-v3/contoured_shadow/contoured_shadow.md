# contoured_shadow

**Tag**: ui, shadow, effects, render-object, widget, mobile

## 简介

Soft, shape-following shadows for Flutter widgets via a RenderObject that blurs the child silhouette. Ideal for cutout images, icons, and irregular shapes.
