# chronoverse

**Tag**: calendar, date-picker, datetime, scheduler, widget, ui

## 简介

All-in-one calendar and date toolkit: month, week, year, schedule and planner views, range and multi pickers, typed input, time zones, recurrence and 80+ date functions.
