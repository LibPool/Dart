# yandex_pay_pay_with_redirect

**Tag**: yandex, payments, fintech, redirect, mobile

## 简介

Yandex Pay "pay with redirect" module for Flutter. Adds the redirect payment flow (Yandex Pay button + async payment-URL bridge) on top of yandex_pay_core.
