# nusa_chat

**Tag**: chat, customer-support, websocket, messaging, web, mobile, networking

## 简介

In-app customer chat for Flutter (Android, iOS) over NusaContact Socket Bridge: text, photos with crop and drawing, documents, location, voice notes and emoji.
