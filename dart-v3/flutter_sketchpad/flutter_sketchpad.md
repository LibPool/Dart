# flutter_sketchpad

**Tag**: drawing, annotation, canvas, image, sketch, mobile

## 简介

Annotate images in Flutter: pressure-sensitive freehand drawing, text, shapes, layers, measurement tools, and sharp vector-rendered PNG export.
