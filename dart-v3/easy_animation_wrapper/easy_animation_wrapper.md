# easy_animation_wrapper

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Elevate your Flutter app's UX effortlessly with animation_wrapper. This package simplifies animation integration, offering customizable fade, slide, and scale effects. Ideal for onboarding screens, page transitions, and UI elements. Enhance your app's visual appeal without the complexity—seamless, polished animations at your fingertips!
