# paddle_wrapper

**Tag**: paddle, payments, checkout, subscriptions, billing, web, mobile

## 简介

Paddle Billing for Flutter web: overlay and inline checkout, typed checkout events, localized price previews, Paddle Retain and payment confirmation.
