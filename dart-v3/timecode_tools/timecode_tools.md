# timecode_tools

**Tag**: timecode, smpte, ltc, audio, midi

## 简介

SMPTE timecode reader. Reads LTC from a stream of PCM audio and MTC from a stream of MIDI bytes: 24, 25, 29.97 drop-frame and 30 fps, lock status and a jitter-free clock. Backed by the timecode-tools Rust core through dart:ffi.
