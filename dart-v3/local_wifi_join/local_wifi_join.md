# local_wifi_join

**Tag**: wifi, hotspot, network, iot, mobile, networking

## 简介

Join and leave a WPA2-Personal Wi-Fi access point, such as an IoT device's own hotspot, on Android and iOS. Reliable on iOS 26+, with native timeouts and explicit result statuses.
