# yandex_pay_quickpay

**Tag**: yandex, payments, fintech, quickpay, mobile, ui

## 简介

Yandex Pay QuickPay module for Flutter. Adds the QuickPay flow (payment methods widget, active payment badge, expandable widget, promo banner) on top of yandex_pay_core.
