# rootfi_tap_to_pay

**Tag**: payments, nfc, fintech, tap-to-pay, cards, web, tooling

## 简介

Official RootFi SDK: NFC phone-to-phone tap-to-pay between RootFi accounts, plus a typed client for the RootFi Cards API. One key, sandbox and live.
