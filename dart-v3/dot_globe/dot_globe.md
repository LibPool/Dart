# dot_globe

**Tag**: globe, map, animation, ui, data-visualization, mobile

## 简介

A dotted (halftone) 3D globe for Flutter — spin it, drop any widget as a lat/lng marker. Pure Dart, zero deps, light & dark presets out of the box.
