# ios_privacy_localizer

**Tag**: ios, localization, permissions, cli, flutter, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Safely localize iOS permission usage descriptions (NS*UsageDescription) for Flutter apps. Atomically updates Info.plist, InfoPlist.strings and pbxproj.
