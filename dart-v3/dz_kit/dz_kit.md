# dz_kit

**Tag**: algeria, geography, phone-number, currency, validation

## 简介

Toolkit for Algerian apps. 69 wilayas and 1541 communes, +213 phone numbers, DZD money in integer centimes, CCP and RIP keys, NIF and NIS checks.
