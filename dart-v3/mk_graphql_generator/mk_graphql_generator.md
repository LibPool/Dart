# mk_graphql_generator

**Tag**: graphql, code-generation, freezed, build-runner, generator, ui, tooling

## 简介

Fast build_runner generator for mk_graphql. Generates Freezed data models, operations, variables, fragments, and combiner queries into .gql.dart files.
