# health_workout_sync

**Tag**: health, healthkit, health-connect, fitness, workout

## 简介

Sync workouts from Apple Health and Health Connect (Samsung Health, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura…) into your backend, in the background, without losing or duplicating any.
