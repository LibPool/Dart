# catalogmx

**Tag**: mexico, validation, fintech, catalogs, sat, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Enterprise Mexican library for Flutter/Dart: validators, generators, tax calculators, CFDI helpers and official SAT/INEGI/SEPOMEX/Banxico/IFT/CNBV catalogs.
