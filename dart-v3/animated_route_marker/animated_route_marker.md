# animated_route_marker

**Tag**: google-maps, maps, marker, animation, tracking, mobile, ui

## 简介

Moves a google_maps_flutter marker smoothly along a route or towards live GPS positions at a real speed, with smooth bearing, play/pause/seek and camera follow.
