# liquidify

**Tag**: ui, glassmorphism, liquid-glass, ios, widgets, mobile

## 简介

Seamless Liquid Glass extension for standard Flutter widgets. Transform standard Material & Cupertino widgets into Apple-style Liquid Glass with .liquidGlass().
