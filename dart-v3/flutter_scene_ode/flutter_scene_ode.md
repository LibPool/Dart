# flutter_scene_ode

**Tag**: physics, ode, flutter-scene, simulation, ffi, web, mobile, desktop

## 简介

ODE (Open Dynamics Engine) physics backend for flutter_scene: rigid bodies, hinge and fixed joints, motors, contacts and raycasts behind the PhysicsSimulation contract of package:scene. Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and web (WebAssembly).
