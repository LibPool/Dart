# celestrak

**Tag**: satellite, tle, omm, orbital, space, mobile, tooling

## 简介

A pure-Dart client for fetching, parsing, and caching satellite TLE, OMM, and SATCAT metadata from CelesTrak and Space-Track. No Flutter dependency.
