# loggerai_flutter

**Tag**: monitoring, crash-reporting, observability, logging, apm, web, mobile, networking

## 简介

LoggerAI for Flutter: crashes and errors grouped with their stack, screens, network calls and a report sheet, linked to your server's requests.
