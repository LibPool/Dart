# mic_recorder

**Tag**: audio, microphone, recording, voice, sound

## 简介

Production-grade microphone recording with PCM streaming, WAV and native AAC/Opus/FLAC/AMR output, dBFS metering, permissions, audio sessions, interruptions and input switching.
