# office_oxide_ffi

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Fast Dart/Flutter FFI for Word, Excel, and PowerPoint: DOCX, XLSX, PPTX plus legacy DOC, XLS, PPT. Extract plain text, Markdown, or HTML via a Rust core. Bindings for office_oxide v0.1.2 (pub patch 0.1.2+1). Not affiliated with Office Oxide or Oxide (oxide.fyi).
