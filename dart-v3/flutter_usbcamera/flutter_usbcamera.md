# flutter_usbcamera

**Tag**: camera, usb, uvc, android, hardware, mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for Android UVC (USB Video Class) camera preview, capture, recording and OpenGL rendering. Based on AndroidUSBCamera (AUSBC).
