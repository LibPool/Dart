# dnp3

**Tag**: dnp3, scada, ffi, industrial, protocol, mobile

## 简介

DNP3 (IEEE 1815) master and outstation for Dart and Flutter on top of the stepfunc dnp3 Rust library, with zero-copy measurements and isolate-friendly handles.
