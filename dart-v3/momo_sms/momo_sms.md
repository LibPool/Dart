# momo_sms

**Tag**: mobile, ui

## 简介

Automatically detects and parses mobile money SMS transactions (M-PESA, Airtel Money, MIXX by YAS, HaloPesa). Zero-loss capture works even when the app is killed or backgrounded. Built for East African MNOs.
