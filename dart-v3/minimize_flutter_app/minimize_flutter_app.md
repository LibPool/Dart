# minimize_flutter_app

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

A Flutter plugin that allows you to minimize your Flutter application (move it to background) on Android devices.

## 官网

- 主页: https://github.com/godwin5/minimize_flutter_app
- pub.dev: https://pub.dev/packages/minimize_flutter_app
