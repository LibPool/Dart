# flutter_welcome_kit

**Tag**: onboarding, tutorial, tooltip, walkthrough, ui, mobile

## 简介

A beautiful, customizable onboarding/tour guide kit for Flutter apps with spotlight overlays, animations, and step-by-step tooltips.
