# just_graphics_engine

**Tag**: graphics, gltf, game-engine, gamedev, flutter-gpu, mobile, ui

## 简介

3D renderer for Flutter on Flutter GPU: glTF 2.0 models, PBR materials, lights, shadows, skinned animation, image-based light and a scene widget.
