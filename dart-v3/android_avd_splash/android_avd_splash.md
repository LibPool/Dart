# android_avd_splash

**Tag**: android, splash-screen, lottie, animation, codegen, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Generates an Android 12+ splash screen from a Lottie file, Animated SVGs or an Animated GIFs: a verified AnimatedVectorDrawable, its resources, and the activity wiring.
