# paycross_flutter

**Tag**: payments, checkout, three-ds, mobile

## 简介

PayCross payment SDK for Flutter: card payments, 3-D Secure v2, saved cards, Google Pay on Android and Apple Pay on iOS, wrapping the native Android and iOS SDKs.
