# flame_multiplayer_dashwire

**Tag**: game-development, flame, networking, multiplayer, web

## 简介

A dashwire connection as a flame_multiplayer wire: rooms, rollback play, turns and ghosts over dashwire's WebSocket, loopback or simulated network, reliable and unreliable delivery kept apart.
