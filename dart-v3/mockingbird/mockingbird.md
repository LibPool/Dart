# mockingbird

**Tag**: audio, voice-activity-detection, vad, ffi, speech, web, mobile, desktop, testing

## 简介

Native audio capture with multilingual voice detection, an anomaly gate, YAMNet sound classification, RNNoise and Opus packets over dart:ffi, on Android, iOS, macOS and web.
