# liquid_glass_fx

**Tag**: liquid-glass, glassmorphism, blur, shader, ui, mobile

## 简介

A pure-Dart Flutter package that renders iOS 26-style liquid glass (refraction, edge highlights, blur and press/drag deformation) with a FragmentShader and BackdropFilter.
