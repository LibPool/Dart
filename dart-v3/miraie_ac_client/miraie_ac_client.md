# miraie_ac_client

**Tag**: mqtt, iot, air-conditioner, panasonic, smart-home, security, tooling

## 简介

A production-ready Dart SDK for controlling Panasonic MirAIe air conditioners over MQTT. Supports authentication, device discovery, real-time status streaming, and full AC control.
