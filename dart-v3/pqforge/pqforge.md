# pqforge

**Tag**: cryptography, post-quantum, security, encryption, flutter, web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart post-quantum crypto for Dart, Flutter, and Serverpod: ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, AES-256-GCM, ChaCha20-Poly1305, hybrids, streaming, and a CLI.
