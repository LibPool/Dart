# dot_shorthand_converter

**Tag**: mobile, tooling

## 简介

A helper package/CLI that automatically refactors common Flutter enum usages (e.g., MainAxisAlignment.start) into the new Dart/Flutter 3.38 dot-shorthand form (.start).
