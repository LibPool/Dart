# skio_uvc_camera

**Tag**: camera, usb, uvc, webcam, android, web, mobile

## 简介

USB (UVC) cameras in Flutter on Android and web: live preview, JPEG capture, snapshot button and resolution choice for endoscopes, microscopes and webcams.
