# vezmo_checkout

**Tag**: payments, checkout, subscriptions, google-pay, apple-pay, mobile

## 简介

The Vezmo payment sheet for Flutter apps: cards with 3D Secure, Google Pay, Apple Pay, pay-later methods, UPI and Pix, in the buyer's own currency. Payments and subscriptions.
