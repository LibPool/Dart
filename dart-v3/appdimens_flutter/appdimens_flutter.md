# appdimens_flutter

**Tag**: responsive, ui, dimensions, scaling, cross-platform, mobile, tooling

## 简介

AppDimens 3.2 — Smart and mathematically refined responsive dimensioning for Flutter. Scaled (sdp/hdp/wdp/ssp) plus 12+ scaling strategies, orientation inverters, facilitators, builders, snapshot-partitioned cache and fast-lane resolution. Flutter port of the AppDimens Dynamic / AppDimens KMP family.
