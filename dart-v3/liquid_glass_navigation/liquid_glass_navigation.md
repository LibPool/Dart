# liquid_glass_navigation

**Tag**: navigation, bottom-navigation-bar, glassmorphism, ui, widget, mobile

## 简介

iOS 26 style Liquid Glass navigation for Flutter. A floating glass bottom bar, a glass side rail and an adaptive scaffold with a stretchy, draggable pill.
