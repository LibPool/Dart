# flywheel_carousel

**Tag**: web, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

A free-spinning, arc-shaped carousel for Flutter. Cards ride the rim of a large invisible wheel; flicks coast under friction and snap to the nearest card. Builder-driven and theme-agnostic — bring your own card content.
