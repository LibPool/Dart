# rune_cupertino

**Tag**: rune, bridge, cupertino, ios, widgets, mobile, ui

## 简介

Cupertino (iOS-style) bridge for the rune package; registers CupertinoButton, CupertinoPageScaffold, and other Cupertino widgets plus CupertinoThemeData and CupertinoIcons on a RuneConfig.
