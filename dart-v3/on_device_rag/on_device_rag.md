# on_device_rag

**Tag**: rag, llm, ai, embeddings, on-device, mobile, ui, tooling

## 简介

Pure-Dart on-device RAG for Flutter and Dart. Chunk text, embed it, search vectors and build grounded LLM prompts, fully offline. Bring your own LLM.
