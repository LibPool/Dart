# gravix_rtc

**Tag**: webrtc, video-call, audio, realtime, rtc, web, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Real-time video and audio rooms for Flutter on Gravix Cloud: one room service, region-aware connect, token providers, join analytics and a video-effect hook.
