# simple_reveal

**Tag**: animation, scrolling, reveal, transition, ui, mobile, state-management

## 简介

Scroll reveal widgets for Flutter, in pure Dart with no dependencies. Blocks fade, slide, zoom, blur, turn or flip into place as they come into view, or with the scroll.
