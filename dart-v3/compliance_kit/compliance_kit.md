# compliance_kit

**Tag**: privacy, security, gdpr, compliance, logging, mobile, testing

## 简介

Privacy and security toolkit for Flutter - PII-redacting logger, GDPR consent, export and deletion, and Play Integrity / App Attest checks with one shared audit trail.
