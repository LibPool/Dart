# waktu_solat_lib

**Tag**: prayer-times, malaysia, islamic, waktu-solat, jakim, web, mobile, tooling

## 简介

Malaysia prayer times API client for Flutter. Fetch waktu solat by zone or GPS, with Imsak & Isyraq.
