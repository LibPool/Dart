# device_integrity

**Tag**: security, attestation, integrity, play-integrity, app-attest, mobile, testing

## 简介

Flutter plugin for device attestation: Google Play Integrity (classic + standard), Huawei SafetyDetect, Apple DeviceCheck and App Attest.
