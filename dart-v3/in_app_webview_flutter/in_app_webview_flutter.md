# in_app_webview_flutter

**Tag**: html, webview, webview-flutter, inappwebview, browser, web, mobile

## 简介

Inline WebView, headless WebView, and in-app browser for Flutter. SPA-safe App Shell, current Flutter/Dart, SPM-ready iOS. No donation banner.
