# atlas3d_flutter

**Tag**: mobile

## 简介

Flutter plugin for immersive 3D rendering on live maps (vehicle avatars, tracking scenes). Maintained fork of thermion_flutter 0.3.4 (Apache-2.0) with App Store native-assets fixes.
