# duo_kit

**Tag**: iphone-duo, foldable, dual-screen, layout, adaptive, mobile, ui

## 简介

iPhone Duo and foldable layouts for Flutter: navigation in the strip, a split view across the fold and dialogs in one half, around your own widgets.
