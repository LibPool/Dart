# condition_aggregator_met_norway

**Tag**: aggregator, advisory, met-norway, norway, winter-driving

## 简介

MET Norway (Meteorologisk institutt) locationforecast adapter for the condition_aggregator interface. Maps Norwegian and Nordic-region weather forecasts to source-neutral Advisory events. Pure Dart.
