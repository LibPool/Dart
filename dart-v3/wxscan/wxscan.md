# wxscan

**Tag**: qr-code, barcode, scanner, image, ffi, mobile

## 简介

QR decoding for Dart and Flutter - a Rust port of the wechat_qrcode algorithm (CNN detection, super resolution). Reads images and raw pixel buffers.
