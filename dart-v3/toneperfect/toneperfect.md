# toneperfect

**Tag**: pronunciation, speech, language-learning, api, sdk, web, tooling

## 简介

Client for the TonePerfect pronunciation assessment API. Sentence, word and sound scores for 10 languages, with Mandarin initial, final and tone per syllable.
