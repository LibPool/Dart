# epub_rtl

**Tag**: epub, ebook, reader, rtl, arabic, mobile

## 简介

Pure-Flutter EPUB 2/3 parser and reader with right-to-left support for Arabic books: page-progression-direction, dir, bidi, paginated RTL paging.
