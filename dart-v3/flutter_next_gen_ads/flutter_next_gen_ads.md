# flutter_next_gen_ads

**Tag**: admob, ads, mobile-ads, android, monetization, mobile

## 简介

Flutter wrapper for Google's GMA Next-Gen SDK 1.0+. Banner, interstitial, rewarded interstitial, and app open ads on Android.
