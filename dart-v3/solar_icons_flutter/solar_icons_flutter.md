# solar_icons_flutter

**Tag**: icons, solar, ui, widgets, mobile

## 简介

The complete Solar Icon Set: 7,759 icons in all six styles (linear, outline, bold, broken, and both duotones) as tree-shakeable icon fonts. No dependencies.
