# trezor_flutter

**Tag**: trezor, hardware-wallet, bluetooth, usb, crypto, mobile, security

## 简介

Trezor hardware wallets in Flutter over native USB and Bluetooth LE, with Codec v1 and the encrypted Trezor-Host Protocol (THP) in pure Dart.
